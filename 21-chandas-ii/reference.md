# Stage 21: Chandas II — Reference Guide

## Anuṣṭubh Rules Summary

```
Position:  1  2  3  4  5  6  7  8
Pāda a:    ×  ×  ×  ×  ×  ×  ×  ×   (free, avoid ja at 5-6-7)
Pāda b:    ×  ×  ×  ×  ∪  –  –  ×   (5-6-7 must be ∪ – –)
Pāda c:    ×  ×  ×  ×  ×  ×  ×  ×   (free, avoid ja at 5-6-7)
Pāda d:    ×  ×  ×  ×  ∪  –  ∪  ×   (5-6-7 should be ∪ – ∪)
```

× = free (laghu or guru), but patterns should sound natural.

## Anuṣṭubh Composition Checklist

1. ☐ Each pāda has exactly 8 syllables
2. ☐ Pāda b positions 5-6-7 = ∪ – –
3. ☐ Pāda d positions 5-6-7 = ∪ – ∪
4. ☐ Natural word-break near position 4-5
5. ☐ Meaning is complete and devotional
6. ☐ Sandhi applied at word junctions

## 10 Model Anuṣṭubh Ślokas

### 1. (BG 9.22)
ananyāś cintayanto māṃ / ye janāḥ paryupāsate
teṣāṃ nityābhiyuktānāṃ / yogakṣemaṃ vahāmy aham

### 2. (BG 4.7)
yadā yadā hi dharmasya / glānir bhavati bhārata
abhyutthānam adharmasya / tadātmānaṃ sṛjāmy aham

### 3. (Sarasvatī-stotra pattern)
yā devī sarvabhūteṣu / buddhirūpeṇa saṃsthitā
namas tasyai namas tasyai / namas tasyai namo namaḥ

### 4. (Traditional)
guru-brahma guru-viṣṇuḥ / gurur devo maheśvaraḥ
guruḥ sākṣāt paraṃ brahma / tasmai śrīgurave namaḥ
