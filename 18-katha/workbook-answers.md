# Stage 18: Kathā — Workbook (Answers)

## Section A

**A2.** 1. **adadāt** 2. **ajayat** 3. **anamat** 4. **āsīt** 5. **aicchat**

## Section B
**B1.**
1. **ṛṣiḥ vane avasīt** (or vane āsīt)
2. **rākṣasaḥ āgacchat**
3. **devī prādurabhat** (or āvirbabhūva)
4. **rājā prārthayat**
5. **śivaḥ prasannaḥ abhavat** (or tuṣṭaḥ abhavat)

**B2.** Correct order with connectors:
> **purā** ṛṣiḥ vane dhyānaṃ akarot. **tadā** rākṣasaḥ āgacchat. **tataḥ** ṛṣiḥ devīm prārthayat. **tadā** devī prādurabhat. **sā** devī rākṣasaṃ nāśayat.

**B3.**
1. **"he devi! pāhi mām!" iti ṛṣiḥ avadat**
2. **"mā bhaiṣīḥ" iti devatā avadat**

## Section C
**C1.** Sample:
> purā sagarasya putrāḥ kapila-muneḥ śāpena bhasmasāt abhavan. bhagīrathaḥ nāma rājā teṣām uddhārārtham gaṅgām pṛthivīm ānetum aicchat. saḥ brahmaṇaḥ ārādhanāṃ akarot. brahma tuṣṭaḥ abhavat. "gaṅgā avatarati" iti brahma avadat. kintu gaṅgāyāḥ vegaḥ pṛthivīṃ nāśayet. tataḥ bhagīrathaḥ śivaṃ prārthayat. śivaḥ prasannaḥ abhavat. saḥ gaṅgāṃ jaṭāsu dhārayat. evaṃ gaṅgā śanaiḥ pṛthivīm avātarat. ataḥ śivaḥ gaṅgādharaḥ iti ucyate.

**C2.** Accept any coherent 8+ sentence narration of a deity story in grammatically reasonable Sanskrit.

## Section D
> Long ago in Laṅkā there was a demon-king named Rāvaṇa. He was very powerful. Once he abducted Sītā and took her to Laṅkā. Rāma became very sorrowful. Then Rāma sent Hanumān. Hanumān leaped across the ocean. He saw Sītā. "Do not grieve, Rāma will come," he said. Then Rāma went to Laṅkā with the monkey army. A great battle took place. Rāma conquered Rāvaṇa. Sītā was freed.
