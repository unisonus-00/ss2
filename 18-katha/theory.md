# Stage 18: Kathā — Narration

## Goal
Narrate a deity's story (līlā) in Sanskrit for 2-3 minutes. Master past tense, sequence markers, and narrative structure.

## Kathā Structure

### Opening
> purā kāle eka-smin vane mahān ṛṣiḥ āsīt.
> (Long ago, in a certain forest, there was a great sage.)

### Development
> ekadā saḥ dhyānaṃ kurvan āsīt. tadā ekaḥ rākṣasaḥ āgacchat.
> (Once he was meditating. Then a demon came.)

### Crisis
> rākṣasaḥ ṛṣiṃ hantuṃ aicchat. ṛṣiḥ bhayam anubhavat.
> (The demon wanted to kill the sage. The sage felt fear.)

### Resolution
> tadā devī prādurabhat. sā rākṣasaṃ nāśayat.
> (Then the Goddess appeared. She destroyed the demon.)

### Closing
> ṛṣiḥ devīm praṇamat. "he devi! dhanyaḥ asmi" iti avadat.
> (The sage bowed to the Goddess. "O Goddess, I am blessed," he said.)

## Sample Kathā: Gaṅgāvataraṇa (Descent of Gaṅgā)

> purā bhagīrathaḥ nāma rājā āsīt. tasya pūrvajāḥ sagarasya putrāḥ kapila-muneḥ śāpena bhasma-sāt abhavan. teṣāṃ uddhārārtham gaṅgā pṛthivīm ānayanīyā āsīt.
>
> bhagīrathaḥ tapasyāṃ akarot. brahma tuṣṭaḥ abhavat. "gaṅgā avadiṣyati" iti avadat. kintu gaṅgāyāḥ vegaḥ pṛthivīṃ nāśayet.
>
> tataḥ bhagīrathaḥ śivasya ārādhanāṃ akarot. śivaḥ prasannaḥ abhavat. gaṅgāṃ jaṭāsu dhārayat. tataḥ śanaiḥ śanaiḥ gaṅgā pṛthivīm avātarat.
>
> evaṃ śivasya kṛpayā gaṅgā loke āgacchat. ataḥ śivaḥ gaṅgādharaḥ iti ucyate.

## Direct Speech in Narration

Use **iti** to close quoted speech:
> "he devi! rakṣa mām!" iti ṛṣiḥ prārthayat.
> (The sage prayed, "O Goddess, protect me!")

> "mā bhaiṣīḥ" iti devī avadat.
> ("Don't fear," said the Goddess.)
