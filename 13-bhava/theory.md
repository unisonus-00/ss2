# Stage 13: Bhāva — Emotional Vocabulary

## Goal
Express inner emotional states — gratitude, longing, surrender, wonder, friendship — in Sanskrit without falling back to English. Develop the vocabulary of the heart.

## What is Bhāva?

**Bhāva** (भाव) means "feeling," "state of being," or "emotional disposition." In bhakti, the bhāva toward the deity determines everything — the words you choose, the tone of prayer, the quality of worship.

## The Five Bhāvas of Bhakti

| Bhāva | Meaning | Attitude | Tone |
|-------|---------|----------|------|
| **Śānta** | Peace | Quiet reverence | calm, meditative |
| **Dāsya** | Service | Servant to master | humble, obedient |
| **Sakhya** | Friendship | Friend to friend | intimate, playful |
| **Vātsalya** | Parental love | Parent to child | tender, protective |
| **Mādhurya** | Sweet love | Lover to beloved | passionate, longing |

## Emotional Vocabulary

### Gratitude (Kṛtajñatā)
| Sanskrit | Meaning |
|----------|---------|
| kṛtajñaḥ asmi | I am grateful |
| dhanyaḥ asmi | I am blessed/fortunate |
| anugrahaḥ | grace, favor |
| kṛpā | mercy, compassion |
| prasādaḥ | grace, clarity |
| anugrāhya me | I am the recipient of grace |
| tava kṛpayā | by your grace |
| dhanyavādaḥ | thanks |

### Longing (Viraha / Utkaṇṭhā)
| Sanskrit | Meaning |
|----------|---------|
| virahaḥ | separation |
| utkaṇṭhā | intense longing |
| tṛṣṇā / pipāsā | thirst |
| kāṅkṣā | desire, yearning |
| ākulaḥ / ākulā | agitated |
| vyākulaḥ | distressed |
| tvāṃ vinā na jīvāmi | without you I cannot live |
| kadā darśanaṃ bhavati? | when will I have your darśana? |
| hṛdayam tvāṃ smṛtvā kampate | my heart trembles remembering you |
| tava virahāt dahyate me manaḥ | my mind burns from separation from you |

### Surrender (Śaraṇāgati / Prapatti)
| Sanskrit | Meaning |
|----------|---------|
| śaraṇam | refuge |
| prapatti | surrender |
| samarpayāmi | I offer/dedicate |
| ātma-nivedanam | self-offering |
| tavāsmi | I am yours |
| tvam eva mama sarvasvam | you alone are my everything |
| ananya-śaraṇaḥ | having no other refuge |
| tvayy arpita-manaḥ | mind offered to you |
| mama kiñcit nāsti | I have nothing (of my own) |

|----------|---------|
| vismayaḥ | wonder, amazement |

### Friendship/Intimacy (Sakhya)
| Sanskrit | Meaning |
|----------|---------|
| sakhe! | O friend! |

## The Game: "One Deity, Five Bhāvas"

Express the same devotion to Śiva through all five emotional lenses:

**Śānta:** śivaḥ sarvam, sarvaṃ śivaḥ. śāntaṃ dhyāyāmi.
(Śiva is all, all is Śiva. I meditate in peace.)

**Dāsya:** he prabho! aham tava dāsaḥ. kiṃ karavāṇi? ājñāṃ dehi.
(O Lord! I am your servant. What shall I do? Give the command.)

**Sakhya:** he śiva! sakhe! adya kiṃ kurvāvaḥ? kailāse viharāvaḥ!
(O Śiva! Friend! What shall we do today? Let's wander on Kailāsa!)

**Vātsalya:** he bāla śambho! ehi, kṣīraṃ piba. tvāṃ rakṣāmi.
(O child Śambhu! Come, drink milk. I will protect you.)

**Mādhurya:** he prāṇanātha! tava vinā hṛdayam śūnyam. kadā āgacchasi?
(O lord of my life! Without you my heart is empty. When will you come?)

## Milestone Check

Can you express your inner state — joy, sorrow, longing, gratitude, or wonder — entirely in Sanskrit, without reverting to English even in thought?
