## Five Bhāvas — Expression Templates

### Śānta (Peace)
- oṃ śāntiḥ śāntiḥ śāntiḥ
- sarvam brahmamayam jagat
- śāntam hṛdayam mama
- nityam ānanda-svarūpam asi tvam

### Dāsya (Service)
- he prabho! aham tava dāsaḥ
- ājñāṃ dehi — kiṃ karavāṇi?
- tava pādau mama śiraḥ
- tava sevāyāṃ mama jīvitam

### Sakhya (Friendship)
- he sakhe! ehi, kathayāvaḥ
- tava hāsena mama hṛdayaṃ pūrṇam
- tvayā saha sarvaṃ sundaram
- mā gaccha — tiṣṭha mayā saha

### Vātsalya (Parental Love)
- he bāla! ehi, mama krodaṃ āgaccha
- mā bhaiṣīḥ — aham atra asmi
- kṣīraṃ piba, modakaṃ khāda
- tvāṃ rakṣāmi sadā

### Mādhurya (Sweet Love)
- he priyatame! kadā āgacchasi?
- tava virahāt hṛdayaṃ dahyate
- tava vadanam eva mama candra
- tvāṃ vinā na kiñcit priyam
