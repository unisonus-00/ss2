# Stage 13: Bhāva — Workbook (Answers)

## Section A
**A1.** 1. **utkaṇṭhā** (or virahaḥ)  2. **śaraṇāgatiḥ** (or prapatti)  3. **vismayaḥ** (or adbhutam)  4. **kṛtajñatā**  5. **śāntiḥ**

**A2.** 1. separation/longing  2. grace/blessing  3. servitude  4. wonder/amazement  5. love

## Section B
**B1.** Samples:
1. **ānando me hṛdaye bhavati** (joy arises in my heart)
2. **duḥkham anubhavāmi** (I experience sorrow)
3. **bhayam asti mama hṛdaye** (fear is in my heart)
4. **dhanyaḥ asmi tava kṛpayā** (I am blessed by your grace)
5. **tava darśanasya utkaṇṭhā mama asti** (I have longing for your vision)

**B2.** Sample: **aho! kīdṛśaṃ tava rūpam! vismayena mama manaḥ pūrṇam.** (Oh! What is your form like! My mind is full of wonder.)

**B3.** Sample: **he prabho! tvām eva śaraṇam prapadye. mama sarvaṃ tvayi samarpayāmi.** (O Lord, I take refuge in you alone. I offer everything to you.)

## Section C
Samples for Śiva:

**C1.** Śānta: **oṃ namaḥ śivāya. sarvam śāntam, sarvam śivam.**
**C2.** Dāsya: **he prabho! aham tava dāsaḥ. ājñāṃ dehi, kāryaṃ karomi.**
**C3.** Sakhya: **he śiva sakhe! adya kailāse viharāvaḥ. tvayā saha ānandaḥ.**
**C4.** Vātsalya: **he bāla śambho! ehi mama kroḍam. tvāṃ rakṣāmi sadā.**
**C5.** Mādhurya: **he prāṇeśvara! tvāṃ vinā hṛdayaṃ śūnyam. kadā milāvaḥ?**

Award 2 marks per bhāva: 1 for correct bhāva tone, 1 for grammatical correctness.

## Section D
**D1.** Sample:
> adya aham devyāḥ mandiraṃ gacchāmi. dvāre sthitvā harṣeṇa pūrṇaḥ bhavāmi. garbhagṛhe devīṃ paśyāmi — aho! kīdṛśī śobhā! mama netrābhyāṃ ānandasya aśrūṇi patanti. kṛtajñaḥ asmi tava kṛpāyai he ambe!

**D2.**
> O Goddess! By your darśana my heart is full. Tears of joy fall from my eyes. Oh! What is your compassion like! I am wretched, but you are full of compassion. At your feet I offer everything of mine.
