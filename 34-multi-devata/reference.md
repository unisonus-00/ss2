# Stage 34: Multi-devatā — Reference Guide

## Deity Vocabulary Quick Cards

### Śiva
Core: hara, śambhu, trilocana, gaṅgādhara, nīlakaṇṭha, naṭarāja
Objects: triśūla, ḍamaru, bhasm, rudrākṣa, kapāla
Places: kailāsa, śmaśāna, mandākiṇī
Consort: pārvatī, umā, gaurī
Vehicle: nandī (bull)

### Viṣṇu/Kṛṣṇa
Core: hari, govinda, mādhava, keśava, acyuta, janārdana
Objects: cakra, śaṅkha, gadā, padma, veṇu (Kṛṣṇa), kodaṇḍa (Rāma)
Places: vaikuṇṭha, vṛndāvana (K), ayodhyā (R), kṣīrasāgara
Consort: lakṣmī, rādhā (K), sītā (R)
Vehicle: garuḍa

### Devī/Durgā
Core: durgā, caṇḍikā, ambikā, mahiṣamardinī, bhagavatī
Objects: khaḍga, triśūla, cakra, dhanuḥ, aṣṭāyudha
Places: vindhya, mahiṣapura
Vehicle: siṃha

### Lalitā/Tripurasundarī
Core: lalitā, rājarājeśvarī, tripurasundarī, kāmeśvarī, bālā
Objects: ikṣu-dhanuḥ (sugarcane bow), puṣpa-bāṇa (flower arrow), pāśa, aṅkuśa
Places: śrīnagara, maṇidvīpa, cintāmaṇi-gṛha
Consort: kāmeśvara

### Sarasvatī
Core: vāgdevī, bhāratī, śāradā, vāṇī, vāgīśvarī
Objects: vīṇā, pustaka, akṣamālā, kamaṇḍalu
Color: śveta (white)
Vehicle: haṃsa

### Gaṇeśa
Core: gaṇapati, vighneśvara, lambodara, ekadanta, gajanāna
Objects: modaka, paraśu, pāśa, aṅkuśa, danta
Vehicle: mūṣaka (mouse)

### Hanumān
Core: mārutātmaja, vāyuputra, añjanīsuta, rāmadāsa, pavananandana
Feats: laṅkā-dāha, sañjīvanī, samudra-laṅghana, sītā-anveṣaṇa

### Sūrya
Core: āditya, bhāskara, divākara, savitā, prabhākara
Objects: ratha, saptāśva (7 horses), prabhā-maṇḍala
Consorts: chāyā, saṃjñā
