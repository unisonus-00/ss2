    Welcome to Abhyāsa!

    Learn Sanskrit through practice, from your first words to composition, poetry, Vedic recitation, and Avadhāna.

    Abhyāsa follows a 36-stage path. Each lesson introduces a new ability, then gives you several ways to make it your own. Card types include recall, multiple choice and sequence.

    Material you master returns through Abhyāsa Review, helping earlier lessons stay active as you progress.

    Open the menu at the top left and tap a stage name — Nāma, Sandhi, Rūpa — and the stage tells you what it gives you, how it runs, and which list to start with. While you practise, the red line under an answer explains the grammar, and the book icon opens the lesson's reference.
