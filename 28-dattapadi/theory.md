# Stage 28: Dattapadī — Forced Vocabulary

## Goal
Compose a stotra that naturally integrates four supplied words — words that may seem completely unrelated to devotion. This tests lexical creativity and the ability to find connections.

## What is Dattapadī?

**Datta** = given. **Padī** = having words/feet. Four words are supplied (often from everyday or incongruous domains). You must compose a 4-line verse where each line contains one of the given words, and the whole verse forms a coherent devotional composition.

## The Challenge

Given words might be: **megha** (cloud), **siṃha** (lion), **nauka** (boat), **dīpa** (lamp)

These seem unrelated to each other or to devotion. But:
- megha → complexion of Viṣṇu is like a cloud
- siṃha → Narasiṃha avatāra
- nauka → the boat that crosses the ocean of saṃsāra
- dīpa → lamp of knowledge

## Techniques for Integration

### 1. Upamā (Simile)
Use the word in a comparison:
> tava rūpaṃ **megha** iva nīlam (your form is dark like a cloud)

### 2. Rūpaka (Metaphor)
Make the word a metaphor:
> saṃsāra-sāgare bhaktiḥ **nauka** iva (devotion is a boat in the ocean of saṃsāra)

### 3. Direct Attribute
Connect the word to a known deity attribute:
> **siṃha**-rūpaḥ narasiṃhaḥ (lion-formed Narasiṃha)

### 4. Philosophical Bridge
Abstract the word:
> **dīpa** iva jñānaṃ tamo nāśayati (like a lamp, knowledge destroys darkness)

### 5. Narrative Context
Place it in a story:
> **megha** garjati, indraḥ varṣati (the cloud thunders, Indra rains)

## Example Solution

Given: **megha, siṃha, nauka, dīpa**

> **megha**-śyāmaṃ nārāyaṇam āśraye
> yaḥ **siṃha**-rūpo daityān nihanti sadā
> saṃsāra-sāgare bhaktiḥ **nauka** mama
> tava jñānaṃ **dīpa** iva tamo harati

Translation: "I take refuge in the cloud-dark Nārāyaṇa / Who in lion-form always slays demons / Devotion is my boat in the ocean of saṃsāra / Your knowledge, like a lamp, removes darkness"

## Practice Method

1. Receive 4 words
2. For each word, brainstorm 3 possible connections to devotion
3. Choose the smoothest connections
4. Compose the verse, one line per word
5. Verify coherence — does the verse tell one story?

## Difficulty Levels

| Level | Word Types |
|-------|-----------|
| Easy | puṣpa, jala, candana, mālā (already devotional) |
| Medium | megha, sindhu, vṛkṣa, tārakā (nature — easy to symbolize) |
| Hard | rathya (street), kūpa (well), mārjāra (cat), kaṭhinya (hardness) |

## Milestone Check
Given 4 arbitrary words, can you compose a coherent devotional verse integrating all four naturally?
