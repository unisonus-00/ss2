# Stage 28: Dattapadī — Workbook (Answers)

## Section A
1. megha: a) **Viṣṇu's complexion** (megha-varṇa) b) **grace raining like a cloud**
2. mārjāra: a) **mārjāra-nyāya** (kitten surrender — prapatti doctrine) b) simile for stealth/māyā
3. kūpa: a) **ajñāna-kūpa** (well of ignorance jīva falls into) b) deep well = depth of devotion
4. ratna: a) **ratnākara** (ocean of jewels) = epithet of deity b) devotion as precious jewel
5. pathika: a) **jīva as traveler** in saṃsāra b) deity as guide for the traveler

## Section B
**B1.** Sample (Viṣṇu):
> **padma**-nābhaṃ prapadye taṃ (I take refuge in lotus-naveled one)
> **candra**-vadanaṃ sundaraṃ harim (moon-faced, beautiful Hari)
> saṃsāra-**sindhu**-tārakaṃ (who helps cross the ocean of saṃsāra)
> jñāna-**agni**-nā tamo harati (who removes darkness with the fire of knowledge)

**B2.** Sample (Śiva):
> kailāsa-**parvata**-nilayaṃ (dwelling on mount Kailāsa)
> **sarpa**-bhūṣaṇa-maṇḍitam (adorned with serpent ornaments)
> gaṅgā-**jala**-dharam śambhum (bearing the water of Gaṅgā)
> bilva-**vṛkṣa**-priyaṃ bhaje (I adore him who loves the bilva tree)

**B3.** Sample (Hard):
> brahma **tantuvāya** iva viśvaṃ vayati (Brahmā weaves the universe like a weaver)
> jīvaḥ **ghaṭa** iva mṛdā nirmitaḥ (the jīva is fashioned like a pot from clay)
> karma-mala-**rajakaḥ** iva guruḥ (the guru is like a washerman of karmic stains)
> saṃsāra-**rathyā**-bhramaṇāt trāhi mām (save me from wandering the street of saṃsāra)

## Section C
- **vaṃśa** → Direct attribute: yadu-vaṃśa-vibhūṣaṇam (ornament of the Yadu lineage)
- **padma** → Direct epithet: padma-nābham (lotus-naveled)
- **siṃha** → Direct form: siṃha-rūpam (lion-formed — Narasiṃha)
- **megha** → Double use: (1) megha-varṇam (direct complexion), (2) megha iva kṛpā (upamā — grace like a cloud)
