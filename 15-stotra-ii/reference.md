## Case-Based Stotra Formulas

### Dative Stack (X-āya namaḥ)
```
[epithet-dat] [epithet-dat] [epithet-dat]
[deity-dat] namaḥ
```

### Instrumental Praise (X-ena / X-ayā)
```
yasya/yasyāḥ [instrument-3rd] [result]
tasmai/tasyai namaḥ
```

### Genitive Glory (X-asya / X-āyāḥ)
```
[deity-gen] [quality] [adjective]
[deity-gen] [quality] [adjective]
```

### Locative Devotion (X-e / X-āyām)
```
[deity-loc] [my state/devotion]
sarvadā [verb]
```

## Useful Phrases by Case

### With Dative (4th)
| Phrase | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| śivāya namaḥ | salutation to Śiva |
| gurave namaḥ | salutation to the guru |
| sarvadevebhyo namaḥ | salutation to all gods |
| namaḥ śivāya | (same, reversed) |

### With Instrumental (3rd)
| Phrase | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| tava kṛpayā | by your grace |
| tava tejasā | by your radiance |
| tava icchayā | by your will |
| bhaktyā muktaḥ | freed through devotion |

### With Ablative (5th)
| Phrase | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| bhayāt rakṣa | protect from fear |
| pāpāt mocaya | free from sin |
| saṃsārāt uddhara | lift from saṃsāra |
| duḥkhāt trāhi | save from sorrow |

### With Genitive (6th)
| Phrase | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| tava rūpam | your form |
| tava māhātmyam | your greatness |
| tava kṛpā | your grace |
| tava nāma | your name |
| yasya smaraṇāt | by remembering whom |

### With Locative (7th)
| Phrase | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| tvayi bhaktiḥ | devotion in you |
| tvayi viśvāsaḥ | trust in you |
| hṛdaye vasasi | you dwell in the heart |
| sarveṣu bhūteṣu | in all beings |
