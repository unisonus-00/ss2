# Stage 15: Stotra II — Varied Cases (Vibhakti Stotram)

## Goal
Compose stotras using intentionally varied vibhaktis. Move beyond the accusative to express devotion through all cases — dative, instrumental, genitive, locative, ablative.

## Case-Centered Stotra Patterns

### Caturthī (Dative) — "To/For X"
The **namaḥ** construction:
> śivāya namaḥ — salutation to Śiva
> viṣṇave namaḥ — salutation to Viṣṇu
> devyai namaḥ — salutation to the Goddess
> guruve namaḥ — salutation to the Guru

Extended: stack dative epithets before namaḥ:
> nīlakaṇṭhāya trilocanāya bhasmāṅgarāgāya maheśvarāya namaḥ

### Tṛtīyā (Instrumental) — "By/Through X"
> śivena rakṣitaḥ — protected by Śiva
> bhaktyā muktaḥ — freed through devotion
> tava kṛpayā jīvāmi — I live by your grace

Stotra pattern: describe the deity's power through instrumental:
> yasya kṛpayā jagatī jayati
> yasya tejasā timiraṃ naśyati

### Pañcamī (Ablative) — "From X"
> śivāt jñānam — knowledge from Śiva
> bhayāt trāhi — save from fear
> pāpāt mocaya — free from sin
> saṃsārāt uddhara — lift from saṃsāra

### Ṣaṣṭhī (Genitive) — "Of X"
> śivasya kṛpā — Śiva's grace
> devyāḥ mahimā — the Goddess's glory
> tava rūpaṃ divyam — your form is divine
> yasya smaraṇāt pāpaṃ naśyati — by remembrance of whom sin is destroyed

### Saptamī (Locative) — "In/On X"
> śive bhaktiḥ — devotion in Śiva
> tvayi viśvāsaḥ — trust in you
> hṛdaye tvaṃ vasasi — you dwell in my heart
> padme lakṣmīḥ — Lakṣmī on the lotus

## The Vibhakti Stotram Game

Compose a stotra where each line deliberately showcases a different case:

### Example: One Deity, Seven Cases
```
śivaḥ jagataḥ pālakaḥ            (1st - Śiva is protector)
śivaṃ smarāmi satatam             (2nd - I remember Śiva)
śivena sarvam rakṣitam             (3rd - by Śiva all is protected)
śivāya sarvasvaṃ dadāmi            (4th - to Śiva I give everything)
śivāt jñānaṃ labhāmahe             (5th - from Śiva we gain knowledge)
śivasya māhātmyaṃ ko vā vaktuṃ śaknoti  (6th - who can tell Śiva's greatness?)
śive mama bhaktiḥ sadā bhavatu     (7th - may my devotion in Śiva always be)
```

## Advanced Technique: Case as Theological Statement

Each case implies a different relationship with the divine:

| Case | Theological Meaning | Example |
|------|-------------------|---------|
| 1st | The divine IS (identity) | śivaḥ sarvam — Śiva is everything |
| 2nd | I seek the divine (object of desire) | śivam bhajāmi |
| 3rd | I am sustained BY the divine | śivena jīvāmi |
| 4th | I dedicate TO the divine | śivāya sarvaṃ samarpayāmi |
| 5th | I come FROM the divine | śivāt aham āgataḥ |
| 6th | I belong to the divine | śivasya dāsaḥ asmi |
| 7th | I rest IN the divine | śive mama viśrāntiḥ |

## Milestone Check
Can you compose a stotra of at least 4 lines, each line built around a different vibhakti?
