## Section C
**C1.** Sample for Devī:
1. (1st) devī mama māta, sarvasya rakṣikā
2. (2nd) devīm smarāmi satatam hṛdaye
3. (3rd) devyā kṛpayā mama jīvanaṃ dhanyam
4. (4th) devyai mama sarvasvaṃ samarpayāmi
5. (5th) devyāḥ caraṇāt amṛtaṃ patati
6. (6th) devyāḥ mahimā vāṇyā na varṇanīyā
7. (7th) devyām mama hṛdayaṃ sthiram tiṣṭhatu

**C2.** Theological significance:
- 1st: She IS the protector (identity/ontology)
- 2nd: She is what I seek (object of devotion)
- 3rd: I live BY her grace (sustaining power)
- 4th: I dedicate TO her (offering/service)
- 5th: From her comes nectar (source of grace)
- 6th: Her glory is beyond words (transcendence)
- 7th: My heart rests IN her (immanence/dwelling)

## Section D
1. nīlakaṇṭhāya namaḥ → **caturthī** (dative) — salutation TO the blue-throated one
2. devyā anugraheṇa jīvāmi → **tṛtīyā** (instrumental) — I live BY the goddess's grace
3. pāpāt trāhi mām → **pañcamī** (ablative) — save me FROM sin
4. śivasya rūpaṃ divyam → **ṣaṣṭhī** (genitive) — Śiva's form is divine (OF Śiva)
5. hṛdaye tvaṃ sadā vasasi → **saptamī** (locative) — you always dwell IN the heart
