# Stage 35: Aṣṭāvadhāna — Integrated Attention (Eight Offerings)

## Goal
Complete a simulated Aṣṭāvadhāna — managing eight simultaneous challenges from eight pṛcchakas, producing correct compositions under all constraints.

## What is Aṣṭāvadhāna?

**Aṣṭa** = eight. **Avadhāna** = attention/concentration. Eight pṛcchakas (questioners) each give you a different challenge. You cycle through them in rounds, attending to each briefly, then moving to the next — holding all eight threads simultaneously.

## The Eight Challenges (Traditional)

| # | Challenge | Skill Tested |
|---|-----------|-------------|
| 1 | **Samasyāpūraṇa** | Given a pāda, complete the verse | Backwards composition |
| 2 | **Dattapadī** | Four given words to integrate | Lexical creativity |
| 3 | **Niṣiddhākṣarī** | Compose without a forbidden letter | Phonological control |
| 4 | **Āśu-kavitva** | Compose on a given theme/deity | Speed composition |
| 5 | **Vācana (Kathā)** | Listen to a story, answer questions later | Auditory memory |
| 6 | **Gaṇanā** | Count bells/claps while composing | Divided attention |
| 7 | **Aprastuta-prasaṅga** | Answer off-topic questions | Wit + memory |
| 8 | **Vyatyasta (Citra)** | Additional constraint (specific meter, etc.) | Multi-constraint |

## The Round Structure

### Round 1:
- Pṛcchaka 1: Gives the samasya pāda → you compose pāda (a)
- Pṛcchaka 2: Gives 4 words → you compose line 1
- Pṛcchaka 3: States forbidden letter → you compose pāda (a) without it
- Pṛcchaka 4: States theme → you compose pāda (a)
- Pṛcchaka 5: Reads part of a story → you listen
- Pṛcchaka 6: Rings bell → you start counting
- Pṛcchaka 7: Asks a question → you answer
- Pṛcchaka 8: States additional constraint → you compose pāda (a)

### Rounds 2-4: Continue each composition, adding one pāda per round.

### Final: Present all compositions + answer story questions + report bell count.

## Simulated Aṣṭāvadhāna Exercise

### Challenge 1 (Samasyāpūraṇa):
Given pāda (d): **viṣaṃ pītvā sukhī bhavat**
Complete the remaining 3 pādas across rounds.

### Challenge 2 (Dattapadī):
Given words: **padma, megha, agni, sindhu**
Compose a 4-line verse, one line per round.

### Challenge 3 (Niṣiddhākṣarī):
Forbidden letter: **na**
Compose a 4-pāda praise of Śiva, no "na" anywhere.

### Challenge 4 (Āśu-kavitva):
Theme: Praise of the Guru
Compose a 4-pāda śloka, one pāda per round.

### Challenge 5 (Kathā):
A short story will be read to you. Answer 3 questions about it at the end.

### Challenge 6 (Gaṇanā):
A bell will ring at random intervals during each round. Report the total at the end.

### Challenge 7 (Aprastuta-prasaṅga):
Random questions will be asked during each round.

### Challenge 8 (Citra):
Compose a verse to Devī in Indravajrā meter (11 syllables per pāda).

## Mental Management

### Partition Strategy
Divide your mind into 8 "slots." Each slot holds:
- The current state (what pāda am I on?)
- The constraint (what rules apply?)
- The content so far (what have I composed?)

### Priority
Focus on the hardest challenges (samasyāpūraṇa, niṣiddhākṣarī) first. Easier ones (gaṇanā, aprastuta) can run in background.

## Milestone Check
Can you complete a simulated Aṣṭāvadhāna with all 8 challenges?
