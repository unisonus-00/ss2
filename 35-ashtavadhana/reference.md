## Round-by-Round Process

```
ROUND 1: Compose pāda (a) for slots 1,2,3,4,8
         Listen to story segment (slot 5)
         Start bell count (slot 6)
         Answer question (slot 7)

ROUND 2: Compose pāda (b) for slots 1,2,3,4,8
         Listen to more story (slot 5)
         Continue count (slot 6)
         Answer question (slot 7)

ROUND 3: Compose pāda (c) for slots 1,2,3,4,8
         Listen to final story segment (slot 5)
         Continue count (slot 6)
         Answer question (slot 7)

ROUND 4: Compose pāda (d) for slots 2,3,4,8
         (Slot 1 pāda d was given)
         Answer story questions (slot 5)
         Report bell count (slot 6)
         Final question (slot 7)
```

## Emergency Recovery

| If you forget... | Do this... |
|-----------------|-----------|
| A pāda you composed | Recall the deity + rasa → reconstruct |
| The bell count | Give your best estimate with confidence |
| A story detail | Answer what you can, acknowledge gaps |
| A constraint | Ask the pṛcchaka to repeat (costs dignity but saves accuracy) |
