# Stage 35: Aṣṭāvadhāna — Workbook (Answers)

## Scoring Rubric

### Challenge 1 — Samasyāpūraṇa (10 marks)
- Verse is coherent and the given pāda fits naturally: 4
- Correct meter (8 syllables per pāda): 3
- Context makes the "strange" pāda sensible: 3
- Sample context: The lotus (padma) — born from water, lives in water

### Challenge 2 — Dattapadī (10 marks)
- All 4 words (siṃha, kamala, vāyu, kāñcana) present: 4
- Natural integration (not forced): 3
- Devotional coherence: 3

### Challenge 3 — Niṣiddhākṣarī (10 marks)
- ZERO occurrences of "ma" (म): 5 (any violation = 0)
- Devotional content about Devī: 3
- Reasonable meter: 2
- Note: all "-am" endings contain "ma" — must rephrase!

### Challenge 4 — Āśu-kavitva (8 marks)
- Relevant to guru-kṛpā theme: 3
- Correct meter: 3
- Poetic quality: 2

### Challenge 5 — Kathā (6 marks)
1. Cow's name: **Kāmadhenu** (2 marks)
2. Who wanted it: **Viśvāmitra** (2 marks)
3. What he became: **Brahmarṣi** (2 marks)

### Challenge 6 — Gaṇanā (4 marks)
Count of "ca": **8** (śivaś ca, viṣṇuś ca, brahmā ca, indraś ca, agniś ca, vāyuś ca, sūryaś ca, candraś ca)
Exact count = 4 marks, off by 1 = 2 marks, off by 2+ = 0

### Challenge 7 — Aprastuta (6 marks)
- Raghuvaṃśa author: **Kālidāsa** (2 marks)
- 19 × 4: **76** (2 marks)
- Gāyatrī from: **Ṛgveda** (2 marks)

### Challenge 8 — Citra (6 marks)
- All 4 pādas begin with "go": 3
- Devotional to Kṛṣṇa: 2
- Correct meter: 1

## Sample Complete Responses

**1.** Samasyā:
> padmaṃ nāma kusumam etat (a)
> sarve jānanti lokāḥ (b)
> tat sundaram atiśobhanam (c)
> **jalāt jātaṃ jale sthitam** (d)

**2.** Dattapadī:
> **siṃha**-rūpaṃ hariṃ bhaje (lion-form Hari)
> **kamala**-nābhaṃ prapadye tam (lotus-naveled)
> **vāyu**-sutena sevitaṃ devam (served by Hanumān)
> **kāñcana**-varṇaṃ nārāyaṇam (gold-complexioned)

**3.** No "ma" (very challenging — even "-am" is blocked!):
> bhaje durgāṃ... wait, durgā**ṃ** has anusvāra not "ma"...
> Actually anusvāra (ṃ) and "ma" (म) are different akṣaras. If only the akṣara "ma" is forbidden:
> durge bhaje tvāṃ sadā śakti (no ma ✓)
> trāhi pāhi dayānidhe (no ma ✓)
> śūla-hastāṃ siṃha-vāhinīṃ (no ma ✓... wait, check every syllable)
> This requires extreme care.

**8.** Go- verse:
> **go**vindaṃ praṇamāmy ahaṃ
> **go**pālaṃ veṇuvādinaṃ
> **go**vardhana-dharaṃ devaṃ
> **go**pī-vallabham āśraye
