## Famous Examples

### Indravajrā (11)
> sā vidyā yā vimuktaye (part of a famous verse)

### Vasantatilakā (14)
> śāntākāraṃ bhujagaśayanaṃ padmanābhaṃ sureśam
(Actually this is Śārdūlavikrīḍita — careful!)

The Viṣṇu dhyāna-śloka:
> śān-tā-kā-raṃ-bhu-ja-ga-śa-ya-naṃ-pa-dma-nā-bhaṃ-su-re-śam
This is 19 syllables = Śārdūlavikrīḍita!

### Mālinī (15)
Many Meghadūta stanzas. Also Devī-māhātmya ślokas.

### Mandākrāntā (17)
Kālidāsa's Meghadūta opening:
> kaś-cit-kān-tā | vi-ra-ha-gu-ru-ṇā | sva-adhi-kā-rāt-pra-ma-ttaḥ
