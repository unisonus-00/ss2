# Stage 22: Chandas III — Multiple Meters

## Goal
Learn 5+ meters beyond anuṣṭubh. Match meter to devotional mood. Compose in several meters.

## The Meters

### 1. Anuṣṭubh (8 per pāda) — review
Pattern: relatively free, 5-6-7 constrained. Mood: narrative, instructional.

### 2. Indravajrā (11 per pāda)
Pattern: **– – ∪ – – ∪ ∪ – ∪ – –** (ta ta ja ga ga)
Mood: Stately, powerful. Good for praising powerful deities.

### 3. Upendravajrā (11 per pāda)
Pattern: **∪ – ∪ – – ∪ ∪ – ∪ – –** (ja ta ja ga ga)
Mood: Flowing, graceful. Mixed with Indravajrā = Upajāti.

### 4. Vasantatilakā (14 per pāda)
Pattern: **– – ∪ – ∪ ∪ ∪ – ∪ ∪ – ∪ – –** (ta bha ja ja ga ga)
Mood: Spring-like, ornate, devotional. Very popular in stotras.

### 5. Mandākrāntā (17 per pāda)
Pattern: **– – – – ∪ ∪ ∪ ∪ ∪ – – ∪ – – ∪ – –** (ma bha na ta ta ga ga)
Yati after 4, 10. Mood: Majestic, slow, meditative. Used for Meghadūta.

### 6. Śārdūlavikrīḍita (19 per pāda)
Pattern: **– – – ∪ ∪ – ∪ – ∪ ∪ ∪ – – – ∪ – – ∪ –** (ma sa ja sa ta ta ga)
Yati after 12. Mood: Grand, elaborate. Most stately of common meters.

### 7. Sragdharā (21 per pāda)
Pattern: **– – – – ∪ – – ∪ ∪ ∪ ∪ ∪ ∪ – – – ∪ – – ∪ –** (ma ra bha na ya ya ya)
Yati after 7, 14. Mood: Most expansive, for supreme praise.

### 8. Mālinī (15 per pāda)
Pattern: **∪ ∪ ∪ ∪ ∪ ∪ – – ∪ – – ∪ – – –** (na na ma ya ya)
Yati after 8. Mood: Gentle, sweet. Ideal for Kṛṣṇa/Devī devotion.

## Matching Meter to Bhāva

| Bhāva | Recommended Meters |
|-------|-------------------|
| Śānta (peace) | Mandākrāntā, Mālinī |
| Vīra (heroic) | Śārdūlavikrīḍita, Indravajrā |
| Śṛṅgāra (beauty) | Vasantatilakā, Mālinī |
| Karuṇa (compassion) | Mandākrāntā, Vasantatilakā |
| Adbhuta (wonder) | Sragdharā, Śārdūlavikrīḍita |
| General praise | Anuṣṭubh, Upajāti |

## Composition Exercise

For each meter, compose just ONE pāda (one line) correctly:

1. **Indravajrā** (11): – – ∪ – – ∪ ∪ – ∪ – –
   > nā-mā-mi-de-vaṃ-tri-pu-rān-ta-kā-ram (check pattern)

2. **Vasantatilakā** (14): – – ∪ – ∪ ∪ ∪ – ∪ ∪ – ∪ – –
   > (compose and scan)

## Key Principle
Start by imitating. Take a famous verse in each meter, replace key words while maintaining the pattern.

## Milestone Check
Can you compose at least one pāda in Indravajrā, Vasantatilakā, and one other meter beyond anuṣṭubh?
