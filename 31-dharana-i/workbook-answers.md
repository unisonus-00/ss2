# Stage 31: Dhāraṇā I — Workbook (Answers)

This stage is primarily practical and self-assessed. There are no fixed "correct" answers — the test is whether you can accurately recall and continue your own compositions.

## Evaluation Criteria

### For Exercises 1 & 2:
- **Recall accuracy** (10 marks per exercise): Did you remember your own pāda (a) correctly after the pause?
- **Continuation quality** (10 marks per exercise): Are pādas b, c, d metrically consistent, thematically connected, and grammatically correct?
- **Completion** (5 marks per exercise): Did you finish all threads?

### Scoring Guide:
| Criterion | Full marks | Half marks | No marks |
|-----------|-----------|------------|----------|
| Recall | Perfect or near-perfect | Remembered theme but not exact words | Forgot entirely |
| Meter | All 8-syllable pādas | Some pādas off by 1 syllable | No metrical awareness |
| Theme | Coherent from a to d | Some drift | Contradictory |
| Grammar | Correct cases and verbs | Minor errors | Major errors |

### For Exercise 3:
Award yourself honestly. The goal is to identify which memory strategies work for YOU.

### Effective strategies commonly reported:
1. **First-word chain** — most common effective strategy
2. **Rhythmic memory** — especially effective for musically trained students
3. **Visual anchoring** — placing each verse in a specific mental location
4. **Semantic story** — remembering what the verse says, then reconstructing

**Total: 60 marks (self-assessed with honesty)**
