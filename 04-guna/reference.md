# Stage 4: Guṇa — Reference Guide

## Adjective Agreement Quick Table

For -a stem adjectives (the most common type):

| Gender | Singular | Example |
|--------|----------|---------|
| Masculine | -aḥ | sundaraḥ devaḥ |
| Feminine | -ā or -ī | sundarī devī |
| Neuter | -am | sundaram vanam |

## The -maya / -vat / -mat Suffixes

| Suffix | Meaning | Example |
|--------|---------|---------|
| -maya | full of, made of | ānandamayaḥ (full of bliss) |
| -vat / -mat | possessing | balavān (possessing strength) |
| -in | having | tejasvī (having tejas) |
