# Stage 4: Guṇa — Adjectives

## Goal
Learn to describe deities and sacred objects with qualities (guṇas). Generate 10 qualities for any deity spontaneously.

## What is a Guṇa?

**Guṇa** (गुण) means "quality" or "attribute." In grammar, adjectives (viśeṣaṇa) describe nouns (viśeṣya). In Sanskrit, adjectives **agree** with their noun in gender, number, and case.

## The Agreement Rule

The adjective takes the same gender, number, and case as the noun it describes.

| Noun | Adjective "beautiful" | Why |
|------|----------------------|-----|
| sundaraḥ devaḥ | masculine -aḥ | devaḥ is masculine |
| sundarī devī | feminine -ī | devī is feminine |
| sundaram phalam | neuter -am | phalam is neuter |
| divyaḥ agniḥ | masculine -aḥ | agniḥ is masculine |
| pavitrā gaṅgā | feminine -ā | gaṅgā is feminine |

## Comparative and Superlative Forms (tara/tama)

Sanskrit forms comparatives and superlatives by adding **-tara** (more) and **-tama** (most) to the adjective stem:

| Base | Comparative (-tara) | Superlative (-tama) | Meaning |
|------|---------------------|---------------------|---------|
| sundara | sundarataraḥ | sundaratamaḥ | beautiful → more beautiful → most beautiful |
| priya | priyataraḥ | priyatamaḥ | dear → dearer → dearest |
| mahān | mahattaraḥ | mahattamaḥ | great → greater → greatest |
| śubha | śubhataraḥ | śubhatamaḥ | auspicious → more auspicious → most auspicious |
| gambhīra | gambhīrataraḥ | gambhīratamaḥ | profound → more profound → most profound |

These forms decline like regular -a stem adjectives and must agree in gender/number/case:

- **Masculine**: sundarataraḥ devaḥ — the more beautiful god
- **Feminine**: sundaratarā devī — the more beautiful goddess
- **Neuter**: sundarataram padmam — the more beautiful lotus

**Devotional usage**: rāmaḥ sarveṣu priyatamaḥ — Rāma is the dearest among all.

The compared entity takes **pañcamī** (ablative) or appears with **sarveṣu** (among all, saptamī):
- viṣṇuḥ śivāt sundarataraḥ — Viṣṇu is more beautiful than Śiva
- devī sarveṣu tejasvitatamā — the Devī is the most radiant among all

## Common Participial Adjectives (-at / -ant forms)

**Present active participles** function as adjectives meaning "one who is doing [verb]." They are formed from the verb stem and decline like adjectives, agreeing with the noun they describe.

### Formation (Parasmaipada present participle):

The suffix **-at** (strong stem **-ant**) is added to the present stem:

| Dhātu | Present Stem | Participle (M nom. sg.) | Meaning |
|-------|-------------|------------------------|---------|
| rakṣ (protect) | rakṣa- | rakṣan / rakṣat | protecting |
| nam (bow) | nama- | naman / namat | bowing |
| gam (go) | gaccha- | gacchan / gacchat | going |
| paṭh (recite) | paṭha- | paṭhan / paṭhat | reciting |
| car (move) | cara- | caran / carat | moving, wandering |
| bhaj (adore) | bhaja- | bhajan / bhajat | adoring |

### Ātmanepada present participle (-māna):

| Dhātu | Participle (M nom. sg.) | Meaning |
|-------|------------------------|---------|
| sev (serve) | sevamānaḥ | serving |
| yaj (sacrifice) | yajamānaḥ | sacrificing |
| man (think) | manyamānaḥ | thinking |

### Gender agreement:

| Gender | Example (rakṣ-) | Meaning |
|--------|-----------------|---------|
| Masculine | rakṣan devaḥ | the protecting god |
| Feminine | rakṣantī devī | the protecting goddess |
| Neuter | rakṣat kulam | the protecting family |

**Devotional usage**:
- rakṣan haraḥ bhaktam pāti — the protecting Hara guards the devotee
- sevamānā bhaktā devīm arcati — the serving devotee worships the Devī
- gacchan muniḥ mandiram paśyati — the walking sage sees the temple

## The -maya Suffix

One of the most productive ways to form adjectives in devotional Sanskrit:

**noun + maya = "full of / made of"**

| Base | + maya | Meaning |
|------|--------|---------|
| karuṇā | karuṇāmayaḥ | full of compassion |
| ānanda | ānandamayaḥ | full of bliss |
| jyotis | jyotirmayaḥ | full of light |
| amṛta | amṛtamayaḥ | nectarean |
| prema | premamayaḥ | full of love |
| tejas | tejomayaḥ | radiant |
| vidyā | vidyāmayaḥ | full of knowledge |
| līlā | līlāmayaḥ | full of divine play |

## The Guṇa-Mālā Game

String together adjective-noun pairs like beads on a garland:

> śivaḥ śāntaḥ — Śiva is peaceful
> devī sundarī — the goddess is beautiful
> kṛṣṇaḥ nīlaḥ — Kṛṣṇa is blue/dark
> gaṅgā pavitrā — Gaṅgā is sacred
> agniḥ tejasvi — fire is radiant
> hanumān vīraḥ — Hanumān is heroic
> sarasvatī vidyāmayī — Sarasvatī is full of knowledge
> rāmaḥ dhīraḥ — Rāma is steadfast

Practice: pick a deity, generate a "garland" of 10 qualities.

## Adjective Placement

In Sanskrit, the adjective can come before or after the noun. Both are correct:
- **sundaraḥ devaḥ** = the beautiful god
- **devaḥ sundaraḥ** = the god is beautiful (or: the beautiful god)

Context determines whether it's attributive or predicative.

### 🧱 New Bricks Learned

| Brick Type | Examples | Snaps onto... |
|------------|----------|---------------|
| Qualifier brick (basic adjective) | sundaraḥ, divyā, nīlam | Noun bricks — must agree in gender, number, and case |
| Suffix brick (-maya, -vat, -in) | ānandamayaḥ, balavān, tejasvī | Noun stems to form new qualifier bricks |
| Comparative brick (-tara) | sundarataraḥ, priyatarā | Adjective stems; compared noun takes pañcamī (ablative) |
| Superlative brick (-tama) | sundaratamaḥ, priyatamā | Adjective stems; used with sarveṣu (among all) |
| Participial brick (-at/-māna) | rakṣan, sevamānaḥ | Verb stems to create adjective-like bricks describing ongoing action |

**Try snapping**: Combine a participial brick with a noun brick and a qualifier brick: "rakṣan divyaḥ devaḥ" (the protecting, divine god). Now add a comparative: "rakṣan sundarataraḥ devaḥ" (the protecting, more-beautiful god).
