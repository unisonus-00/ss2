## Section B: -maya Suffix

**B1.**
1. karuṇā + maya = **karuṇāmayaḥ** (full of compassion)
2. ānanda + maya = **ānandamayaḥ** (full of bliss)
3. jyotis + maya = **jyotirmayaḥ** (full of light)
4. amṛta + maya = **amṛtamayaḥ** (nectarean)
5. prema + maya = **premamayaḥ** (full of love)

**B2.**
1. karuṇāmayaḥ → **karuṇāmayī**
2. ānandamayaḥ → **ānandamayī**
3. jyotirmayaḥ → **jyotirmayī**
4. tejomayaḥ → **tejomayī**
5. premamayaḥ → **premamayī**
