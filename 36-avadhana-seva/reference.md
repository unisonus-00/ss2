# Stage 36: Avadhāna-Sevā — Reference Guide

## Avadhāna Performance Checklist

### Before Performance
- [ ] Invoke iṣṭadevatā mentally
- [ ] Prepare tracking sheet (8 slots)
- [ ] Clear mind — 5 minutes of prāṇāyāma
- [ ] Opening śloka ready

### During Performance
- [ ] Stay in the present — attend fully to the current pṛcchaka
- [ ] Use "bookmark" technique between slots
- [ ] Maintain composure — smile through difficulty
- [ ] Don't rush — accuracy over speed
- [ ] Use constraints as inspiration, not obstacles

### After Performance
- [ ] Present all compositions cleanly
- [ ] Acknowledge errors honestly
- [ ] Thank pṛcchakas
- [ ] Closing invocation

## Self-Assessment Rubric

| Skill | Beginner | Intermediate | Advanced | Avadhānī |
|-------|----------|-------------|----------|----------|
| Vocabulary | 100 words | 500 words | 1000+ words | Unlimited paryāya |
| Declension | 1-2 paradigms | All common | Automatic | Unconscious |
| Meter | Anuṣṭubh only | 3-4 meters | 7+ meters | Any meter |
| Composition speed | 5 min/pāda | 1 min/pāda | 15 sec/pāda | Spontaneous |
| Constraint handling | 1 at a time | 2-3 | 4-5 | 6+ simultaneous |
| Memory threads | 1-2 | 3-4 | 5-6 | 8+ |
| Rasa control | Unconscious | Aware | Intentional | Masterful |
| Recovery from error | Freeze | Pause and recover | Smooth recovery | No errors visible |

## The Tradition's Living Heart

Avadhāna is not a dead art. It lives wherever a poet sits before an audience, receives impossible challenges, and — through grace, practice, and attention — transforms constraint into beauty.

Your journey from nāma (Stage 1) to avadhāna-sevā (Stage 32) is complete as a curriculum. But as practice, it never ends.

> anantā vai vedāḥ — the Vedas are infinite
> anantā vai kavitā — poetry too is infinite

Begin. Continue. Offer.
