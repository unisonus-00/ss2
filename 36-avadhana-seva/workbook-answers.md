# Stage 36: Avadhāna-Sevā — Final Examination (Answers)

## Scoring Rubric (100 marks total)

### 1. Samasyāpūraṇa (15 marks)
- Context makes "mriyate ca sa jīvati" sensible: 5
  - Best contexts: yogī dying to ego, living in brahman; or Śiva drinking poison
- Correct meter throughout: 5
- Poetic quality: 5

### 2. Dattapadī (15 marks)
- All 4 words present (darpaṇa, latā, dīpa, payodhi): 4
- Natural integration: 4
- Devotional coherence: 4
- Poetic quality: 3
- Sample connections: darpaṇa=ātman reflecting brahman, latā=bhakti-vine, dīpa=jñāna-lamp, payodhi=saṃsāra-ocean or karuṇā-ocean

### 3. Niṣiddhākṣarī (15 marks)
- ZERO "ta" (त) violations: 8 (any single violation = max 4)
- Devotional to Śiva: 4
- Poetic quality: 3
- Note: "ta" blocks: tava, asti, -ti endings, triśūla, naṭarāja, satatam... very challenging!

### 4. Āśu-kavitva (10 marks)
- Theme "dawn at temple" expressed: 4
- Correct meter: 3
- Beauty/imagery: 3

### 5. Kathā (9 marks)
a. Where born: **Mathurā** (kāragṛhe in Mathurā) — 3
b. Who carried: **Vasudeva** — 3
c. Mountain lifted: **Govardhana** — 3

### 6. Gaṇanā (6 marks)
Words starting with "a" (अ): aham, adya, atra, āgacchāmi (ā?), aśvena, agamam, api, atra, āsīt (ā?), ālayaḥ (ā?)

If counting only अ (short a): aham, adya, atra, aśvena, agamam, api, atra = **7**
If counting ā (आ) as well: + āgacchāmi, āsīt, ālayaḥ = **10**
Accept either interpretation. Exact count = 6 marks, off by 1 = 4, off by 2 = 2.

### 7. Aprastuta (12 marks, 3 each)
- Kṛṣṇa born in: **Mathurā** (3)
- 144 ÷ 12 = **12** (3)
- Kālidāsa's mahākāvyas: **Raghuvaṃśa** and **Kumārasambhava** (3)
- First word of Ṛgveda: **agnim** (agním īḷe...) (3)

### 8. Citra (18 marks)
- All 4 pādas have "va" alliteration: 6
- About Sarasvatī: 4
- Correct meter: 4
- Beauty: 4
- Sample: vāgdevīṃ vandāmi varade / vīṇā-vādana-viśāradām / vidyā-vilāsa-vibhūṣitām / varadāṃ vāṇīṃ prapadye sadā
