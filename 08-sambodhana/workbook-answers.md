## Section C

**C1.**
1. **he śiva! pāhi mām!** (or rakṣa mām)
2. **he devi! prasīda!**
3. **he kṛṣṇa! dehi me bhaktim!**
4. **he rāma! naya mām!**
5. **he guro! kṣamasva!**

**C2.**
1. **he śiva! tvām namāmi!** (or tubhyaṃ namaḥ)
2. **he ambe! rakṣa mām!** (or he mātar!)
3. **he bhagavan! dehi me jñānam!**
4. **he devi! prasīda!**
5. **tvāṃ śaraṇaṃ prapadye**

**C3.**
1. O Goddess, save me!
2. O Kṛṣṇa, give me love
3. O Lord, show mercy
4. O Śiva, you alone are my refuge
5. O Rāma, be gracious, protect me

**C4.** Sample for Śiva:
1. he śiva! he mahādeva! prasīda!
2. he karuṇānidhe! pāhi mām!
3. he nīlakaṇṭha! dehi me jñānam!
4. he śambho! rakṣa mām sadā!

## Section D

**D1.**
1. rakṣa **mām**
2. dehi **mahyam** (or **me**) jñānam
3. **mama** pāpam kṣamasva
4. **aham** bhaktaḥ asmi
5. **mayi** kṛpāṃ kuru

**D2.** Sample:
1. he devi! rakṣa **mām** — O Goddess, protect me
2. **mama** pāpam kṣamasva — forgive my sin
3. **mayi** kṛpāṃ kuru — show mercy upon me

---

## Section E

Sample prayer to Devī:
1. **he devi! he jagadambe! namāmi tvām** (O Goddess, O Mother of the world, I bow to you)
2. **tvaṃ karuṇāmayī, tvaṃ sundarī** (You are full of compassion, you are beautiful)
3. **pāhi mām he mātar!** (Save me, O Mother!)
4. **dehi me bhaktim, dehi me jñānam** (Give me devotion, give me knowledge)
5. **mama pāpam kṣamasva** (Forgive my sins)
6. **tvām eva śaraṇaṃ prapadye** (To you alone I take refuge)

Award marks for: vocative forms (2), imperative variety (3), pronoun usage (2), praise line (1), surrender line (1), overall coherence (1).
