# Stage 8: Sambodhana — Reference Guide

## Vocative Formation Rules

| Stem Type | Nominative | Vocative | Rule |
|-----------|-----------|----------|------|
| M -a | devaḥ | he deva! | drop visarga |
| F -ā | durgā | he durge! | -ā → -e |
| F -ī | devī | he devi! | -ī → -i |
| M -u | guruḥ | he guro! | -uḥ → -o |
| M -i | agniḥ | he agne! | -iḥ → -e |
| M -an | rājan | he rājan! | unchanged |
| M -at | bhagavat | he bhagavan! | -at → -an |

## Pronoun Quick Reference (aham = I)

| Case | Form | Use in Prayer |
|------|------|--------------|
| 1st | aham | aham bhaktaḥ (I am a devotee) |
| 2nd | mām | rakṣa mām (protect me) |
| 3rd | mayā | mayā kṛtam (done by me) |
| 4th | mahyam / me | dehi me (give to me) |
| 5th | mat | mat sakāśāt (from me) |
| 6th | mama / me | mama pāpam (my sin) |
| 7th | mayi | mayi kṛpā kuru (show mercy to me) |
