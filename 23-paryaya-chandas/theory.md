# Stage 23: Paryāya-Chandas — Lexical Flexibility

## Goal
Develop the ability to swap synonyms instantly to save a meter — making lexical search automatic under metrical pressure.

## The Problem
You're composing in Vasantatilakā and need a 2-syllable word for "sun" at a position requiring – –. "Sūryaḥ" (– –) works! But what if you need ∪ – ? Then "raviḥ" (∪ –). Need ∪ ∪ – ? Try "bhānuḥ" doesn't work... "dinakaraḥ" (∪ ∪ ∪ ∪) — too many lags. "arkaḥ" (– –) — back to guru-guru.

This is the **Save the Meter** game: when your first-choice word doesn't fit the metrical pattern, instantly find a synonym that does.

## The Technique

1. **Know 3-5 synonyms** for every common noun
2. **Know their prosodic profiles** (laghu-guru patterns)
3. **When stuck**, cycle through synonyms mentally: padmam (––), kamalam (∪∪∪), aravindam (∪∪––), paṅkajam (–∪∪)
4. **Pick the one that fits** the required pattern at that position

## Practice Method

Take a half-composed pāda with a gap:
> – – ∪ – – [need ∪ ∪ –] ∪ – – (Indravajrā, 11 syllables)

Position 6-7-8 needs ∪ ∪ –. I need a word for "lotus":
- padmam (– –) — NO
- kamala (∪ ∪ ∪) — close, but last is laghu
- kamale (∪ ∪ –) — YES! (locative gives the guru ending)

**Key insight:** Changing the vibhakti (case form) changes the prosodic pattern too! This gives you even more options.
