# Stage 14: Stotra I — Workbook (Answers)

## Section A
1. **śivam** 2. **devīm** 3. **viṣṇum** 4. **rāmam** 5. **durgām**
6. **śāntam** 7. **divyām** 8. **nityam** 9. **sundarīm** 10. **anantam**

## Section B
**B1.** Samples:
1. **śāntam divyam** namāmi śivam
2. **sundarīm karuṇāmayīm** namāmi devīm
3. **govindam vanamālinam** bhajāmi kṛṣṇam
4. **dhīram dhārmikam** smarāmi rāmam
5. **mahāntam siddhidāyakam** vandāmi gaṇeśam

**B2.** Sample for Śiva:
1. śāntam divyam trilocanam namāmi
2. nīlakaṇṭham umāpatim namāmi
3. gaṅgādharam naṭarājam namāmi
4. kailāsavāsam viśvanātham namāmi

## Section C
**C1.** Accusative words: **acyutam, keśavam, rāma-nārāyaṇam, kṛṣṇa-dāmodaram, vāsudevam, harim, śrīdharam, mādhavam, gopikā-vallabham, jānakī-nāyakam, rāmacandram** — all 11 words are accusative, governed by **bhaje**.

**C2.** Gender-shifted rewrite:
1. **śāntām divyām mahādevīm bhajāmi**
2. **karuṇāmayīm trilocanām bhajāmi**

(Note: trilocanā as F bahuvrīhi is valid. Alternatively use a different epithet: sundarīm jagadambām bhajāmi)

## Section D
**D1.** Sample yaḥ...tam stotra:
> yā sṛṣṭiṃ karoti yā pālayati
> yā saṃhāraṃ kurute jagataḥ
> yā sarvaśaktiḥ yā karuṇāmayī
> tāṃ jagadambāṃ praṇamāmi sadā

**D2.** Sample with smarāmi:
> gaṅgādharam umāsahitam smarāmi
> naṭarājam nṛtyapadam smarāmi
> yogīśvaram dhyānaratam smarāmi
> sadāśivam bhaktavadam smarāmi

Award: correct accusatives (3), valid epithets (2), coherent praise (2), correct verb usage (1).
