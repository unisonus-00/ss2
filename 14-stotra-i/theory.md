# Stage 14: Stotra I — Accusative Constructions (Namāmi Challenge)

## Goal
Compose original praise verses using accusative (dvitīyā) constructions — the most natural structure for devotional poetry. Write a 4-line original stotra.

## The Namāmi Construction

The simplest stotra pattern uses **namāmi** + accusative:
> namāmi śivam — I bow to Śiva

This extends naturally into poetry:
> namāmi śivaṃ śāntam — I bow to peaceful Śiva
> namāmi śivaṃ divyam — I bow to divine Śiva
> namāmi śivaṃ nityam — I bow to eternal Śiva

## Classical Models

### From Śiva-pañcākṣara-stotra (Ādi Śaṅkara):
> nāgendrahārāya trilōcanāya
> bhasmāṅgarāgāya maheśvarāya
> nityāya śuddhāya digambarāya
> tasmai "na"-kārāya namaḥ śivāya

Notice: each line is a stack of **dative** attributes, but the same principle works with accusative.

### From Acyutāṣṭakam:
> acyutam keśavaṃ rāma-nārāyaṇam
> kṛṣṇa-dāmodaraṃ vāsudevaṃ harim
> śrīdharaṃ mādhavaṃ gopikā-vallabham
> jānakī-nāyakaṃ rāmacandram bhaje

Here **bhaje** (I adore) takes a series of accusative names.

## The Namāmi Challenge: 4-Line Composition

### Step 1: Choose your deity and verb
> namāmi ___

### Step 2: Stack accusative descriptions
Each line = adjective(s) in accusative + deity name in accusative + verb

### Template:
```
[adj-acc] [adj-acc] [deity-acc] [verb]
[adj-acc] [adj-acc] [deity-acc] [verb]
[adj-acc] [adj-acc] [deity-acc] [verb]
[adj-acc] [adj-acc] [deity-acc] [verb]
```

### Example for Śiva:
```
śāntam divyam mahādevam namāmi
nīlakaṇṭham trilocanam namāmi
gaṅgādharam umāpatim namāmi
kailāsavāsam jagadīśam namāmi
```

### Example for Devī:
```
sundarīm karuṇāmayīm namāmi devīm
jagadambām mahāmāyām namāmi devīm
durgām bhayahāriṇīm namāmi devīm
śaktim parameśvarīm namāmi devīm
```

## Techniques for Richer Stotras

### 1. Epithet Stacking
> padmanābham, sureshvaram, vanamālinam, garuḍadhvajam — namāmi viṣṇum

### 2. Relative Clause (yaḥ/yā/yat)
> yaḥ śūlapāṇiḥ yaḥ nīlakaṇṭhaḥ
> yaḥ gaṅgādharo yaḥ umāpatiḥ
> tam śaṅkaram... namāmi

### 3. Action Description
> yo nṛtyati yaḥ hasati yaḥ dahati yaḥ sṛjati
> taṃ naṭarājaṃ namāmi

### 4. Refrain (dhruva-pada)
Use the same verb at the end of every line as a refrain.

## Milestone Check
