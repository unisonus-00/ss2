This stage is performance-based. Answers are self-generated and self-timed.

## Scoring Guide

### Section A (3 marks each):
- Under 15s + correct meter + appropriate rasa = 3
- Under 30s + correct meter = 2
- Under 60s + reasonable attempt = 1
- Over 60s or wrong = 0

### Section B (5 marks each):
- All constraints met + under 30s = 5
- All constraints met + under 60s = 4
- One constraint missed = 2
- Multiple constraints missed = 0

### Section C (3 marks each):
- Distinct rasa clearly expressed + correct meter = 3
- Rasa somewhat expressed = 2
- Rasa unclear = 1
Total time benchmark: under 3 minutes for all 5 = excellent

### Section D (5 marks each):
- Exact or near-exact recall = 5
- Theme correct, words differ slightly = 3
- Only remembered the deity/topic = 1
