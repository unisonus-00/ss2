# Stage 25: Rasa — Reference Guide

## Navarasa Vocabulary Banks

### Śānta
śānta, nitya, akhaṇḍa, nirañjana, nirguṇa, nirvikāra, sthira, dhīra, yoga, dhyāna, samādhi, ātman, brahman, mokṣa, turīya, kaivalya, avyaya, acintyam

### Vīra
vijaya, jayati, jitam, parākrama, śūra, bala, vīrya, siṃha, garjana, praharaṇa, khaḍga, cāpa, śara, yuddha, saṃgrāma, raṇa, krodha, abhaya

### Karuṇa
kṛpā, dayā, karuṇā, dīna, ārt, duḥkhita, hā!, trāhi, pāhi, śaraṇa, bandhu, mātar, netra-jala (tears), rudan, vilāpa, snigdha

### Adbhuta
aho!, āścaryam, citraṃ, adbhutam, apūrvam, ko'yam, kīdṛśam, māhātmya, prabhāva, vismaya, akathya, anirvacana, līlā, māyā

### Raudra
ugra, krodha, pralaya, saṃhāra, dahana, jvālā, bhīma, bhayaṅkara, rākṣasa, asura, garjana, huṅkāra, tāṇḍava, kāla

### Śṛṅgāra
sundara, rūpa, lāvaṇya, kānti, kāma, priya, vallabha, līlā, vilāsa, smita, hāsa, manda, kusuma, candana, mālya

### Bhayānaka
bhaya, trāsa, kamp, vepathu, palāyana, ghora, karāla, timira, pretāḥ, piśāca, mṛtyu, yama

## Rasa-Meter Pairings

| Rasa | Best Meters | Reason |
|------|------------|--------|
| Śānta | Mandākrāntā, Mālinī | Slow, meditative |
| Vīra | Śārdūlavikrīḍita, Indravajrā | Powerful, stately |
| Karuṇa | Vasantatilakā, Mandākrāntā | Flowing, emotional |
| Adbhuta | Sragdharā, Śārdūlavikrīḍita | Grand, expansive |
| Śṛṅgāra | Vasantatilakā, Mālinī | Beautiful, ornate |
| Raudra | Śārdūlavikrīḍita | Forceful |

## Rasa Indicators in Composition

| Element | Śānta | Vīra | Karuṇa |
|---------|-------|------|--------|
| Verb mood | Declarative | Imperative | Optative/plea |
| Sentence length | Long, flowing | Short, punchy | Medium, broken |
| Alaṅkāra | Upamā (serene) | Atiśayokti | Virodha, utprekṣā |
| Deity aspect | Meditating | Fighting | Rescuing |
| Reader response | Calm | Inspired | Moved |
