# Stage 25: Rasa — Emotional Aesthetics

## Goal
Consciously control the rasa (aesthetic flavor) of your devotional composition. Describe the same deity in śānta, vīra, adbhuta, karuṇa, and other rasas.

## The Nine Rasas (Navarasa)

| Rasa | Sthāyibhāva (root emotion) | Mood | Color |
|------|---------------------------|------|-------|
| Śṛṅgāra | Rati (love) | Romantic, beautiful | Green |
| Hāsya | Hāsa (laughter) | Comic, playful | White |
| Karuṇa | Śoka (grief) | Compassionate, sad | Grey |
| Raudra | Krodha (anger) | Furious, wrathful | Red |
| Vīra | Utsāha (heroism) | Heroic, brave | Orange |
| Bhayānaka | Bhaya (fear) | Terrifying | Black |
| Bībhatsa | Jugupsā (disgust) | Revolting | Blue |
| Adbhuta | Vismaya (wonder) | Wondrous, magical | Yellow |
| Śānta | Śama (peace) | Serene, transcendent | White/clear |

## Rasa in Devotional Context

Different rasas reveal different facets of the same deity:

### Śiva in Five Rasas

**Śānta (Peace):**
> dhyāna-sthitaḥ śāntaḥ sadāśivaḥ / nirañjanaḥ nirguṇaḥ nirvikāraḥ
> (Meditative, peaceful, eternal Śiva — stainless, beyond qualities, unchanging)

**Vīra (Heroic):**
> tripurāntakaḥ saṃhāra-mūrtiḥ / triśūla-dhārī raudra-naṭarājaḥ
> (Destroyer of Tripura, embodiment of dissolution, trident-wielding, fierce cosmic dancer)

**Adbhuta (Wonder):**
> aho! ardhanaṭeśvaraḥ! ardhaṃ strī ardhaṃ puruṣaḥ! / ko'yaṃ māhātmyam!
> (Oh! The half-woman lord! Half female, half male! What is this greatness!)

**Karuṇa (Compassion):**
> kṛpāluḥ śivaḥ duḥkhitānāṃ śaraṇam / dīnānāṃ bandhuḥ, ārtānām trātā
> (Compassionate Śiva, refuge of the suffering, friend of the wretched, rescuer of the distressed)

**Śṛṅgāra (Beauty):**
> candraśekharaḥ sumukhāḥ / pārvatyā saha kailāse ramate
> (Moon-crested, beautiful-faced, he sports with Pārvatī on Kailāsa)

## Rasa Control Techniques

### Vocabulary Selection
| Rasa | Choose words like... |
|------|---------------------|
| Śānta | śānta, nirañjana, akhaṇḍa, nitya, sthira |
| Vīra | jayati, vijaya, parākrama, śūra, bala |
| Karuṇa | kṛpā, dayā, dīna, ārt, trāhi, duḥkha |
| Adbhuta | aho!, adbhuta, āścarya, citraṃ, apūrva |
| Raudra | krodha, ugra, pralaya, dahana, garjana |

### Meter Selection
Śānta → slow meters (Mandākrāntā). Vīra → powerful meters (Śārdūlavikrīḍita). Karuṇa → flowing meters (Vasantatilakā).

### Sentence Structure
Śānta → simple declaratives. Vīra → exclamatory, active verbs. Karuṇa → pleas, conditionals. Adbhuta → questions, exclamations.

## The Game: "Change the Darśana"

Given one deity, rapidly switch between rasas — same deity, different emotional lens. The words, meter, alaṅkāras, and sentence structures all change.

## Milestone Check
Can you compose 4 short passages about one deity, each in a consciously different rasa, and explain what makes each passage that rasa?
