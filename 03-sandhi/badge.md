# Stage 3 Badge: Sandhikāra

**Title:** Joint Master
**Badge:** 🏅 Sandhikāra

## Requirements

1. Given 10 word-pairs, correctly apply the right sandhi rule and name it
2. Given 5 combined forms, split them and identify the sandhi
3. Explain the difference between ac, hal, and viśeṣa sandhi with one example each

## How to Demonstrate

**Format:** Live drill in voice channel or text post

Post your submission in **#badge-submissions** on Discord. A reviewer will verify and grant the badge.

## Unlock

Completing this badge unlocks Stage 4 content and grants the **Sandhikāra** role on Discord.

## Drill Deck

For daily spaced-repetition drilling of every sandhi in this stage — utsarga vs apavāda, the seven ac sandhis (including Ayavāyāva and Lopaḥ Śākalyasya), the ten visarga rules, the consonant rules, and the two pada-anta rules (Na-Lopa 8.2.7, Ku-Tvam 8.2.30) — see cards **SS2-048 → SS2-086** in [`../flashcards/esg2-flashcards.csv`](../flashcards/esg2-flashcards.csv). Two synthesis cards (SS2-085 for `namaḥ + te → namaste`, SS2-086 for `mahā + īśvara → maheśvara`) walk a full pair-trace and are good final-check drills before you attempt the badge.
