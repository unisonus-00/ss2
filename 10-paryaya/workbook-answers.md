# Stage 10: Paryāya — Workbook (Answers)

## Section A
**A1.**
1. Śiva: **śambhuḥ, śaṅkaraḥ, haraḥ, mahādevaḥ, rudraḥ** (accept any valid)
2. Lotus: **padmam, kamalam, aravindam, paṅkajam, sarasijam**
3. Sun: **sūryaḥ, raviḥ, arkaḥ, bhāskaraḥ, ādityaḥ**

## Section B
**B1.**
1. Nīlakaṇṭha = **nīla** (blue) + **kaṇṭha** (throat) → blue-throated one
2. Gaṅgādhara = **gaṅgā** + **dhara** (√dhṛ, bearer) → bearer of Gaṅgā
3. Trilocana = **tri** (three) + **locana** (eye) → three-eyed
4. Padmanābha = **padma** (lotus) + **nābha** (navel) → lotus-naveled
5. Caturbhuja = **catur** (four) + **bhuja** (arm) → four-armed
6. Tripurasundarī = **tripura** (three cities/worlds) + **sundarī** (beautiful woman) → beauty of the three worlds
7. Jagadambā = **jagat** (world) + **ambā** (mother) → mother of the world
8. Annapūrṇā = **anna** (food) + **pūrṇā** (full) → she who is full of food
9. Gopāla = **go** (cow) + **pāla** (√pā, protector) → protector of cows
10. Vṛṣabhadhvaja = **vṛṣabha** (bull) + **dhvaja** (banner) → bull-bannered

**B2.**
1. Hara ← **√hṛ** (to take/remove)
2. Bhāskara ← **√kṛ** (to make) [bhās + kara = maker of light]
3. Durgā ← **√gam** (to go) [dur + gā = hard to approach]
4. Śaṅkara ← **√kṛ** (to make) [śam + kara = maker of peace]
5. Divākara ← **√kṛ** (to make) [divā + kara = maker of day]

## Section C
**C1.**
1. padmam → **aravindam** (4 syllables)
2. sūryaḥ → **arkaḥ** (2 syllables)
3. jalam → **vāri** (2 syllables)
4. candraḥ → **śaśī** is 2; use **niśākaraḥ** for 4 or **induḥ** for 2. For 3: **niśāpaḥ** or accept **candramāḥ**
5. agniḥ → **vahniḥ** (2) or **analaḥ** (3 syllables)

**C2.**
- 3 syllables: **sindhuḥ** or **abdhiḥ** (2) — better: **sā-ga-raḥ** (3)
- 4 syllables: **sa-mu-dra-ḥ** (3-4) or **u-da-dhi-ḥ** (4)
- 5 syllables: **rat-nā-ka-ra-ḥ** (4-5)

**C3.** Synonyms are essential because Sanskrit meters require specific syllable counts and weight patterns (guru/laghu). If "padmam" doesn't fit the meter, "kamalam" or "aravindam" might. Example: in anuṣṭubh, if you need 3 syllables for "lotus," use "kamalam"; if you need 4, use "aravindam."

**C4.** Sample new epithets for Śiva:
1. **jñānadāyaka** (giver of knowledge)
2. **bhaktarakṣaka** (protector of devotees)
3. **śvetavṛṣabha** (white-bulled)
4. **yogīśvara** (lord of yogīs)
5. **kailāsavāsin** (dweller of Kailāsa)
