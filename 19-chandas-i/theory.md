# Stage 19: Chandas I — Syllables

## Goal
Understand the syllable system (akṣara, laghu/guru) that underlies all Sanskrit meter. Learn to hear and mark 8-syllable units.

## Laghu and Guru

Every Sanskrit syllable is either **laghu** (light, ∪) or **guru** (heavy, –):

**Laghu (∪):** a short vowel (a, i, u, ṛ) NOT followed by a conjunct consonant or visarga/anusvāra
**Guru (–):** a long vowel (ā, ī, ū, e, ai, o, au) OR a short vowel followed by conjunct consonant, anusvāra (ṃ), or visarga (ḥ)

### Rules
1. Long vowel → always guru: **nā** (–), **de** (–), **vai** (–)
2. Short vowel + single consonant + vowel → laghu: **na-ma** (∪ ∪)
3. Short vowel + conjunct → guru: **bha-ktyā** (–, because k+t follows)
4. Short vowel + anusvāra → guru: **saṃ** (–)
5. Short vowel + visarga → guru: **śi-vaḥ** (last syllable –)
6. Last syllable of a pāda is counted as guru by convention

### Examples
| Word | Syllables | Pattern |
|------|-----------|---------|
| namāmi | na-mā-mi | ∪ – ∪ |
| śivāya | śi-vā-ya | ∪ – ∪ |
| bhagavān | bha-ga-vān | ∪ ∪ – |
| triśūlam | tri-śū-lam | ∪ – – |
| namaḥ | na-maḥ | ∪ – |
| padmam | pad-mam | – – |

## Gaṇas (Metrical Feet)

Groups of 3 syllables have names:

| Gaṇa | Pattern | Memory | Example |
|------|---------|--------|---------|
| ya | ∪ – – | yamātā | yamātā |
| ma | – – – | mātārā | mātārā |
| ta | – – ∪ | tārāja | tārāja |
| ra | – ∪ – | rājabhā | rājabhā |
| ja | ∪ – ∪ | jabhalā | jabhalā |
| bha | – ∪ ∪ | bhānasa | bhānasa |
| na | ∪ ∪ ∪ | nasala | nasala |
| sa | ∪ ∪ – | salāgā | salāgā |

**Memory trick:** Take the phrase **ya-mā-tā-rā-ja-bhā-na-sa-la-gā-m**. Each gaṇa's pattern is read from its starting letter through the next two syllables.

## 8-Syllable Units: The Anuṣṭubh Foundation

The most common meter in Sanskrit is **anuṣṭubh** (śloka) — 4 pādas of 8 syllables each, total 32.

Learn to feel groups of 8:
> na-mā-mi-śi-va-māṃ-śān-tam (8 syllables)
> Count: 1-2-3-4-5-6-7-8

Practice clapping on each syllable while reciting any śloka.

## The Mantra Tetris Game

Take a devotional phrase and reshape it to fit exactly 8 syllables:

| Phrase | Syllables | Adjustment |
|--------|-----------|------------|
| namāmi śivam | 5 | too short — add: śāntam → namāmi śivam śāntam (7)... nityam → 8! |
| namāmi nīlakaṇṭham | 7 | add: tam → 8... or: namāmi nīlakaṇṭha(m) prabhu(m) |

This is the beginning of metrical composition.
