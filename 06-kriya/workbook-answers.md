# Stage 6: Kriyā — Workbook (Answers)

## Section A: Conjugation

**A1.**
1. nam, 1st sing. → **namāmi**
2. pūj, 3rd sing. → **pūjayati**
3. gam, 2nd sing. → **gacchasi**
4. dā, 1st sing. → **dadāmi**
5. dṛś, 3rd pl. → **paśyanti**

**A2.** bhaj:
- 3rd: **bhajati**
- 2nd: **bhajasi**
- 1st: **bhajāmi**

**A3.** smṛ:
- 3rd: **smarati**
- 2nd: **smarasi**
- 1st: **smarāmi**

**A4.** Parasmaipada ("for another") uses -ti/-si/-mi endings; ātmanepada ("for oneself") uses -te/-se/-e endings. Parasmaipada example: **namati** (nam). Ātmanepada example: **sevate** (sev).

## Section B: Translation

**B1.**
1. I bow to Śiva → **śivam namāmi** (or namāmi śivam)
2. I sing a hymn → **stotram gāyāmi**
3. I go to the temple → **mandiram gacchāmi**
4. I offer a flower → **puṣpam arpayāmi**
5. I meditate on the deity → **devam dhyāyāmi**

**B2.**
1. bhaktaḥ devam pūjayati → **The devotee worships the deity**
2. mantram japāmi → **I chant a mantra**
3. śivaḥ rakṣati → **Śiva protects**
4. dīpam jvālayāmi → **I light a lamp**
5. gāyāmi stotram → **I sing a hymn**

**B3.**
1. namāmi → **namati**
2. gacchāmi → **gacchati**
3. dadāmi → **dadāti**
4. paśyāmi → **paśyati**
5. karomi → **karoti**

## Section C: Sentence Building

**C1.**
1. aham devam **pūjayāmi**
2. bhaktaḥ mandiram **gacchati**
3. tvam mantram **japasi**
4. devī bhaktam **rakṣati**
5. vayam stotram **gāyāmaḥ**

**C2.** Sample answers:
1. **prātaḥ uttiṣṭhāmi** (I rise in the morning)
2. **snānam karomi** (I bathe)
3. **mandiram gacchāmi** (I go to the temple)
4. **dīpam jvālayāmi** (I light a lamp)
5. **puṣpam arpayāmi** (I offer a flower)

Accept any 5 grammatically correct sentences with different verbs.

## Section D: Negation & Questions

**D1.**
1. jānāmi → **na jānāmi** (I do not know)
2. paśyāmi → **na paśyāmi** (I do not see)
3. tyajāmi → **na tyajāmi** (I do not abandon)

**D2.**
1. na tyajāmi devam → **I do not abandon the deity**
2. kiṃ paśyasi? → **What do you see?**

Sample answer:
