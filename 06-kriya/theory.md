# Stage 6: Kriyā — Verbs

## Goal
Learn Sanskrit verbs to describe acts of worship, devotion, and daily spiritual practice. Describe your own worship in Sanskrit.

## What is a Kriyā?

**Kriyā** (क्रिया) means "action." Sanskrit verbs conjugate according to:
- **Puruṣa** (person): prathama (3rd), madhyama (2nd), uttama (1st)
- **Vacana** (number): eka (singular), dvi (dual), bahu (plural)
- **Lakāra** (tense/mood): laṭ (present), loṭ (imperative), laṅ (past), etc.

## Present Tense (Laṭ-lakāra) — The Essential Tense

### Parasmaipada (active) endings:

| Person | Singular | Dual | Plural |
|--------|----------|------|--------|
| Prathama (he/she/it) | -ti | -taḥ | -anti |
| Madhyama (you) | -si | -thaḥ | -tha |
| Uttama (I) | -mi | -vaḥ | -maḥ |

| Dhātu | 1st person | Meaning | Devotional Use |
|-------|-----------|---------|----------------|

| pūj (10P) | pūjayāmi | I worship | pūjayāmi devīm |
| smṛ (1P) | smarāmi | I remember | smarāmi kṛṣṇam |
| dṛś (1P) | paśyāmi | I see | paśyāmi mūrtim |
| dā (3P) | dadāmi | I give | dadāmi puṣpam |
| śru (5P) | śṛṇomi | I hear | śṛṇomi mantram |

| sthā (1P) | tiṣṭhāmi | I stand | tiṣṭhāmi mandire |

## All Three Persons

Example with "nam" (to bow):

| Person | Singular | Meaning |
|--------|----------|---------|
| 3rd | namati | he/she bows |
| 2nd | namasi | you bow |
| 1st | namāmi | I bow |

Example with "pūj" (to worship, causative/10th class):

| Person | Singular | Meaning |
|--------|----------|---------|
| 3rd | pūjayati | he/she worships |
| 2nd | pūjayasi | you worship |
| 1st | pūjayāmi | I worship |

## Ātmanepada Verbs

Some verbs use ātmanepada (middle voice) endings:

| Person | Singular | Dual | Plural |
|--------|----------|------|--------|
| 3rd | -te | -ete | -ante |
| 2nd | -se | -ethe | -dhve |
| 1st | -e | -vahe | -mahe |

Key ātmanepada verb: **sev** (to serve) → **seve** (I serve)

## The Game: "What Do I Offer?"

Describe your daily worship using verbs:

> prātaḥ uttiṣṭhāmi (in the morning I rise)
> snānaṃ karomi (I bathe)
> mandiram gacchāmi (I go to the temple)
> devam paśyāmi (I see the deity)
> puṣpam arpayāmi (I offer a flower)
> dhūpam dadāmi (I give incense)
> dīpam jvālayāmi (I light a lamp)
> mantram japāmi (I chant a mantra)
> stotram gāyāmi (I sing a hymn)
> devam namāmi (I bow to the deity)
> dhyāyāmi (I meditate)
> prasādam svīkaromi (I accept prasāda)

## Past Tense (Laṅ-lakāra — Imperfect)

The imperfect tense describes completed past actions. It is formed by:
1. Adding the **augment a-** prefix to the verb stem
2. Using **laṅ endings** (secondary endings)

### Parasmaipada Laṅ endings:

| Person | Singular | Dual | Plural |
|--------|----------|------|--------|
| Prathama (he/she/it) | -at | -atām | -an |
| Madhyama (you) | -aḥ | -atam | -ata |
| Uttama (I) | -am | -āva | -āma |

### Paradigm: √bhū (to be/become)

| Person | Singular | Dual | Plural |
|--------|----------|------|--------|
| 3rd | abhavat | abhavatām | abhavan |
| 2nd | abhavaḥ | abhavatam | abhavata |
| 1st | abhavam | abhavāva | abhavāma |

### Paradigm: √nam (to bow)

| Person | Singular | Dual | Plural |
|--------|----------|------|--------|
| 3rd | anamat | anamatām | anaman |
| 2nd | anamaḥ | anamatam | anamata |
| 1st | anamam | anamāva | anamāma |

### Paradigm: √pūj (to worship, 10th class)

| Person | Singular | Dual | Plural |
|--------|----------|------|--------|
| 3rd | apūjayat | apūjayatām | apūjayan |
| 2nd | apūjayaḥ | apūjayatam | apūjayata |
| 1st | apūjayam | apūjayāva | apūjayāma |

### Paradigm: √rakṣ (to protect)

| Person | Singular | Dual | Plural |
|--------|----------|------|--------|
| 3rd | arakṣat | arakṣatām | arakṣan |
| 2nd | arakṣaḥ | arakṣatam | arakṣata |
| 1st | arakṣam | arakṣāva | arakṣāma |

**The augment pattern**: Notice the **a-** prefix on every form. This is the hallmark of the laṅ-lakāra. When the dhātu already begins with a vowel, the augment merges with it (vṛddhi): e.g., √iṣ → aicchat.

**Devotional usage**:
- bhaktaḥ devam apūjayat — the devotee worshipped the deity
- śivaḥ jagat arakṣat — Śiva protected the world
- aham mandiram agaccham — I went to the temple
- vayam stotram agāyāma — we sang a hymn

## Imperative (Loṭ-lakāra)

The imperative mood is essential for prayers, commands, and blessings. It expresses direct requests to the deity.

### Parasmaipada Loṭ endings:

| Person | Singular | Dual | Plural |
|--------|----------|------|--------|
| Prathama (let him/her) | -tu | -tām | -antu |
| Madhyama (you!) | -(a)/-(hi) | -tam | -ta |
| Uttama (let me) | -āni | -āva | -āma |

### Paradigm: √bhū (to be/become)

| Person | Singular | Dual | Plural |
|--------|----------|------|--------|
| 3rd | bhavatu | bhavatām | bhavantu |
| 2nd | bhava | bhavatam | bhavata |
| 1st | bhavāni | bhavāva | bhavāma |

### Paradigm: √nam (to bow)

| Person | Singular | Dual | Plural |
|--------|----------|------|--------|
| 3rd | namatu | namatām | namantu |
| 2nd | nama | namatam | namata |
| 1st | namāni | namāva | namāma |

### Paradigm: √pūj (to worship, 10th class)

| Person | Singular | Dual | Plural |
|--------|----------|------|--------|
| 3rd | pūjayatu | pūjayatām | pūjayantu |
| 2nd | pūjaya | pūjayatam | pūjayata |
| 1st | pūjayāni | pūjayāva | pūjayāma |

### Paradigm: √rakṣ (to protect)

| Person | Singular | Dual | Plural |
|--------|----------|------|--------|
| 3rd | rakṣatu | rakṣatām | rakṣantu |
| 2nd | rakṣa | rakṣatam | rakṣata |
| 1st | rakṣāṇi | rakṣāva | rakṣāma |

**Devotional usage — prayers and commands**:
- rakṣa mām — protect me!
- āgaccha — come!
- pāhi mām — save me! (√pā, 2nd sg. imperative: pāhi)
- bhagavan prasīda — O Lord, be gracious!
- devāḥ rakṣantu naḥ — may the gods protect us
- stotram paṭha — recite the hymn!
- dhyānam kuru — do meditation! (√kṛ imperative: kuru)

## Optative (Vidhiliṅ-lakāra) — "Should" / "May"

The optative expresses wishes, duties, and gentle suggestions. It uses the mood marker **-yā-** (or **-ī-** in parasmaipada) inserted before the personal ending.

### Parasmaipada Vidhiliṅ endings:

| Person | Singular | Dual | Plural |
|--------|----------|------|--------|
| 3rd | -et | -eyātām | -eyuḥ |
| 2nd | -eḥ | -eyātam | -eta |
| 1st | -eyam | -eva | -ema |

### Key devotional forms:

| Dhātu | 3rd sg. | 1st sg. | Meaning |
|-------|---------|---------|---------|
| pūj | pūjayet | pūjayeyam | one should worship / I should worship |
| rakṣ | rakṣet | rakṣeyam | one should protect / may he protect |
| nam | namet | nameyam | one should bow |
| bhaj | bhajet | bhajeyam | one should adore |
| smṛ | smaret | smareyam | one should remember |

**Devotional usage**:
- nityam devam pūjayet — one should worship the deity daily
- sarve janāḥ dharmaṃ bhajeyuḥ — may all people follow dharma
- bhaktaḥ satatam smaret — the devotee should always remember
- rakṣet dharmam sadā — one should always protect dharma

## Negation

To negate a verb, place **na** before it:
- na jānāmi — I do not know
- na paśyāmi — I do not see
- na tyajāmi — I do not abandon

## Milestone Check

Can you describe your own daily worship (real or ideal) using at least 8 different verbs?

Can you retell yesterday's pūjā using the past tense? Can you write 3 prayers using the imperative?

### 🧱 New Bricks Learned

| Brick Type | Examples | Snaps onto... |
|------------|----------|---------------|
| Present verb brick (laṭ) | namāmi, pūjayati | Subject (prathamā) + object (dvitīyā) noun bricks |
| Past verb brick (laṅ) | anamam, apūjayat | Same noun bricks, but describes completed actions; adds a- prefix |
| Command verb brick (loṭ) | rakṣa, āgaccha, pāhi | Direct address — snaps onto vocative noun bricks for prayers |
| Wish verb brick (vidhiliṅ) | pūjayet, rakṣet | Expresses duty/wish — "one should..." or "may he..." |

**Try snapping**: Take the sentence "aham devam namāmi" (present). Now swap the verb brick to past: "aham devam anamam" (I bowed to the deity). Now to imperative, addressing the deity: "he deva! mām rakṣa!" (O deity, protect me!). Now to optative: "bhaktaḥ devam namet" (the devotee should bow to the deity).
