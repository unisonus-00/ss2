# Stage 16: Prārthanā — Workbook (Answers)

## Section A
**A1.** 1. **pāhi** 2. **dehi** 3. **kuru** 4. **kṣamasva** 5. **śṛṇu**
**A2.** 1. **pātu** 2. **bhavatu** 3. **jayatu** 4. **rakṣatu** 5. **karotu**

## Section B
**B1.** 1. **dehi** 2. **kṣamasva** 3. **trāhi** (or pāhi) 4. **naya** 5. **śṛṇu**

**B2.** Sample: dehi me jñānam / dehi me bhaktim / dehi me śāntim / dehi me balam / dehi me muktim

**B3.** Sample: bhayāt trāhi mām / pāpāt rakṣa mām / duḥkhāt trāhi mām / ajñānāt rakṣa mām / saṃsārāt uddhara mām

## Section C
**C1.** Sample prayer to Devī:
> he devi! he jagadambe!
> śṛṇu mama prārthanām.
> mama pāpaṃ kṣamasva.
> mama ajñānaṃ nāśaya.
> dehi me jñānam, dehi me bhaktim.
> bhayāt trāhi mām, duḥkhāt rakṣa mām.
> tava pādayoḥ mama manaḥ sthāpaya.
> tvām eva śaraṇaṃ prapadye.

**C2.** Sample:
> śivaḥ pātu mama śiraḥ / devī rakṣatu mama hṛdayam
> gaṇeśaḥ nayatu mama mārgam / sarasvatī bodhayatu mama buddhim
> sarve devāḥ mama jīvanaṃ maṅgalaṃ kurvantu

## Section D
**D1.**
1. Lead me from untruth to truth
2. O Goddess, destroy my sorrow
3. May all be happy
4. By your grace, may all be auspicious
5. O Lord of the world, give me discrimination

**D2.**
1. **he śiva! sadā mām pāhi** (or rakṣa mām sadā)
2. **devī jayatu**
3. **dehi me śāntim, dehi me jñānam**
4. **ajñānāt trāhi mām** (or ajñānāt rakṣa mām)
5. **sarve prāṇinaḥ sukhinaḥ bhavantu** (or sarve bhūtāḥ sukhinaḥ bhavantu)
