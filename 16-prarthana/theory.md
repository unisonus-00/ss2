# Stage 16: Prārthanā — Requests and Imperatives

## Goal
Compose original prayers with imperatives, optatives, and benedictions. Master the grammar of asking gracefully — from urgent plea to gentle request.

## Degrees of Request

### 1. Direct Imperative (Loṭ) — Command/Urgent Plea
> pāhi mām! — save me!
> rakṣa mām! — protect me!
> dehi me jñānam! — give me knowledge!

### 2. Optative (Liṅ / Āśīrliṅ) — Wish/Gentle Request
> bhūyāt — may it be
> syāt — may there be
> pātu — may [he/she] protect
> jayatu — may [he/she] be victorious

### 3. Potential (Vidhi-liṅ) — Should/Ought
> kuryāt — one should do
> bhajet — one should worship
> paṭhet — one should recite

### 4. First Person Optative — "Let me..."
> bhavāni — let me be
> karāṇi — let me do
> seveyam — let me serve

## Prayer Structures

### Structure 1: Vocative + Imperative + Object
> he devi! dehi me bhaktim
> O Goddess, give me devotion

### Structure 2: Vocative + Description + Imperative
> he karuṇāsindho! mama duḥkhaṃ nāśaya
> O ocean of compassion, destroy my suffering

### Structure 3: Purpose Clause (yathā... tathā kuru)
> yathā tvāṃ na vismarāmi tathā kuru
> Make it so that I never forget you

### Structure 4: Conditional Request (yadi... tadā)
> yadi me doṣāḥ santi tadā kṣamasva
> If I have faults, then forgive

### Structure 5: Benedictive Chain
> śivaḥ rakṣatu, devī pālayatu, guruḥ bodhayatu
> May Śiva protect, may Devī preserve, may Guru enlighten

## Complete Sample Prayer

```
he jagadīśvara! he karuṇāsāgara!
śṛṇu mama prārthanām!

mama pāpaṃ kṣamasva,
mama duḥkhaṃ nāśaya,
mama ajñānaṃ dūrīkuru.

dehi me jñānam,
dehi me bhaktim,
dehi me śāntim.

tava pādayoḥ mama manaḥ sthāpaya.
tava nāmni mama jihvāṃ niyojaya.
tava rūpe mama cakṣuḥ sthirīkuru.

sarvadā tvayi mama cittam bhavatu.
tvām eva śaraṇaṃ prapadye.
```
