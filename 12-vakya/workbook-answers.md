# Stage 12: Vākya — Workbook (Answers)

## Section A
**A1.**
1. **aham prātaḥ uttiṣṭhāmi** (I get up in the morning)
2. **bālaḥ jalam pibati** (the child drinks water)
3. **mātā gṛhe bhojanaṃ pacati** (mother cooks food at home)
4. **aham sāyam mandiraṃ gacchāmi** (I go to the temple in the evening)
5. **guruḥ śiṣyān pāṭhayati** (the guru teaches students)

**A2.**
1. **aham gṛhe paṭhāmi**
2. **bhaktaḥ puṣpeṇa pūjayati**
3. **devī bhaktam rakṣati**
4. **aham prātaḥ mandiram gacchāmi**
5. **guruḥ śiṣyāya kathāṃ kathayati**

**A3.**
1. **aham paṭhāmi likhāmi ca**
2. **yadā sūryaḥ udeti tadā tārakāḥ na dṛśyante**
3. **tvam gacchasi, aham tu tiṣṭhāmi**
4. **yaḥ bhaktaḥ saḥ mucyate** (or yaḥ bhajati saḥ mucyate)
5. **prātaḥ uttiṣṭhāmi, tataḥ snānam karomi**

## Section B
**B1.**
1. **tvam kutra vasasi?**
2. **aham na jānāmi**
3. **mātā gṛhe bhojanaṃ pacati**
4. **prātaḥ sūryaḥ udeti**
5. **kaḥ āgacchati?**

**B2.**
1. Today I do not go to the temple
2. My guru is a great knower/wise one
3. Where is your house?
4. She meditates every day
5. If you worship, then liberation happens

**B3.** Sample:
1. **prātaḥ aham uttiṣṭhāmi**
2. **mukhaṃ prakṣālayāmi**
3. **snānam karomi**
4. **devam namāmi**
5. **prātarāśam karomi**

## Section C
**C1.** Sample:
> mama gṛham sundaram asti. gṛhe pañca kakṣāḥ santi. ekā kakṣā mama pūjā-gṛham asti. tatra śivasya mūrtiḥ asti. pratidinṃ aham tatra pūjām karomi.

**C2.** Sample:
> he śiva! adya prātaḥ aham uttiṣṭhāmi. snānaṃ karomi. tava mandiraṃ gacchāmi. puṣpaṃ arpayāmi. mantram japāmi. tataḥ gṛham āgacchāmi. pustakam paṭhāmi. madhyāhne bhojanaṃ karomi. sāyam punaḥ tvāṃ smarāmi. rātrau śāntyā svapimi.

Award marks for: variety of verbs (3), correct cases (3), flow/coherence (2), vocabulary range (2).
