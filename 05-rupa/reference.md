## Devotional Declension Examples

### śiva (M, -a stem)
| Vib. | Form | Usage |
|------|------|-------|
| 1 | śivaḥ | śivaḥ rakṣati (Śiva protects) |
| 2 | śivam | śivam namāmi (I bow to Śiva) |
| 3 | śivena | śivena saha (with Śiva) |
| 4 | śivāya | śivāya namaḥ (salutation to Śiva) |
| 5 | śivāt | śivāt jñānam (knowledge from Śiva) |
| 6 | śivasya | śivasya kṛpā (Śiva's grace) |
| 7 | śive | śive bhaktiḥ (devotion in Śiva) |
| 8 | he śiva! | he śiva prasīda! (O Śiva, be gracious!) |
