# Stage 0 Badge: Devanāgarī — Script Literacy

## Mastery Target

Decode ordinary Sanskrit Devanāgarī reliably enough that the script is no
longer what stands between you and the vocabulary.

## Requirements

1. **The letters.** Read all 13 independent vowel letters and all 33
   consonant letters aloud, in any order, without hesitating.

2. **The vowel signs.** Read a consonant with any of the vowel signs on it,
   and say where each sign sits — including that the i sign is written before
   its consonant and read after it.

3. **The built-in a.** Given a word, say which consonants carry a vowel that
   is not written, and which carry none. Read a word ending in a virāma
   correctly: पुष्पम्, not puṣpama. Read हृ, रु and रू, where the vowel sign
   sits where the ordinary rule would not put it.

4. **The look-alikes.** Read both members of each of the eight confusable
   sets on sight: भ / म, ब / व, थ / य, ख / रव, घ / ध, प / ष, ङ / ड, उ / ज.

5. **Conjuncts.** State the productive rule — the first consonant loses its
   upright stroke, or stacks its partner underneath where it has no stroke to
   lose — and split nine ordinary conjuncts into their parts, including one
   of three consonants. Read क्ष, ज्ञ, त्त, क्त, त्र, श्व, श्च and श्र by
   sight, which are the only conjuncts you are asked to learn as shapes.

6. **र in both positions.** Read कर्म and क्रम correctly, and say what
   distinguishes them on the page.

7. **The marks.** Name and read the anusvāra, the visarga, the virāma and
   the daṇḍa.

8. **Transfer.** Read aloud, correctly, five Sanskrit words you have never
   seen before — for example क्षेत्रम्, गर्भगृहम्, प्रदक्षिणा, ऋत्विक्,
   उत्तिष्ठ — without being told what any of them means.

## What Is Not Required

Handwriting. Reciting the alphabet in order. Memorising a list of conjuncts.
Knowing where in the mouth any of these sounds is made — that is Varṇa-Vidyā,
and it is a different badge.

## Demonstration

Open a Sanskrit text you have not read before and read a full verse aloud
without stopping over any shape. If nothing on the page brings you to a halt,
the badge is earned.
