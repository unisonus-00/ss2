# Stage 33: Aprastuta-prasaṅga — Workbook (Questions)

## Exercise 1: Compose Under Interruption (30 marks)

Begin composing a śloka to Śiva. After EACH pāda, answer the interrupting question before continuing.

**Pāda a:** Compose → ___

INTERRUPTION: How many kāṇḍas are in the Rāmāyaṇa? Answer: ___

**Pāda b:** Continue from pāda a → ___

INTERRUPTION: What is 13 × 7? Answer: ___

**Pāda c:** Continue → ___

INTERRUPTION: Why does Śiva wear a serpent? Give a witty answer: ___

**Pāda d:** Complete the śloka → ___

Write the complete śloka without the interruptions: ___

## Exercise 2: Dual-Task (15 marks)

While mentally reciting "oṃ namaḥ śivāya" continuously, answer these:
1. Name 3 rivers of India: ___
2. What is 256 ÷ 8? ___
3. Name the 5 pāṇḍavas: ___
4. Who wrote the Meghadūta? ___
5. What is the 4th vibhakti called? ___

Were you able to maintain the mental recitation throughout?
