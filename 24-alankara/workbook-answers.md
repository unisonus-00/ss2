# Stage 24: Alaṅkāra — Workbook (Answers)

## Section A
**A1.**
1. **Upamā** (simile — "iva" = like)
2. **Rūpaka** (metaphor — called "ocean of compassion" directly)
3. **Anuprāsa** (alliteration of ś-)
4. **Atiśayokti** (hyperbole — fame fills all directions)
5. **Virodha** (paradox — cool by fire)

**A2.** Upameya: **mukham** (face). Upamāna: **candra** (moon). Sādhāraṇa-dharma: **bhāti/śobhā** (shining/beauty). Vācaka: **iva** (like).

## Section B
**B1.** Sample upamā: **tava kṛpā gaṅgā iva pavitrā** (your grace is pure like the Gaṅgā)
**B2.** Sample rūpaka: **he karuṇā-sindho!** (O ocean of compassion!) or **jñāna-dīpa śiva** (Śiva, lamp of knowledge)
**B3.** Sample anuprāsa: **śāntaṃ śubhaṃ śivaṃ śaṅkaraṃ bhaje** (alliteration of ś)
**B4.** Sample utprekṣā: **tava netraṃ nīlotpalam iva vikasati** (your eye blooms as if it were a blue lotus)

## Section C
**C1.**
Plain: namāmi devīṃ karuṇāmayīṃ sundarīṃ pavitrām
Adorned:
> **candra-mukhīṃ** [rūpaka] karuṇā-**sindhu**-samānāṃ [rūpaka + upamā]
> **mā**yā-hariṇīṃ **mā**ngalya-**mā**linīṃ [anuprāsa of m]
> devīṃ namāmi

**C2.** Sample 4-line stotra:
1. candra-vadanaṃ [rūpaka] devam namāmi (face-as-moon)
2. śambhuṃ śaṅkaraṃ śāntaṃ śivam [anuprāsa: ś]
3. yasya kṛpā gaṅgā iva pavitrā [upamā: grace like Gaṅgā]
4. taṃ karuṇā-sāgaraṃ prapadye [rūpaka: ocean of compassion]
