# Stage 24: Alaṅkāra — Poetic Ornament

## Goal
Transform correct verse into *beautiful* verse using alaṅkāras (figures of speech). Master upamā (simile), rūpaka (metaphor), anuprāsa (alliteration), and other ornaments.

## What is Alaṅkāra?

**Alaṅkāra** (अलङ्कार) means "ornament." Just as jewelry adorns the body, alaṅkāras adorn poetry. A verse can be grammatically correct and metrically perfect but still lack beauty — alaṅkāra provides the beauty.

## Major Alaṅkāras

### 1. Upamā (Simile) — "like"
Explicit comparison using iva, yathā, sadṛśa, tulya, etc.

> tava mukhaṃ candraḥ **iva** śobhate
> (Your face shines **like** the moon)

> gaṅgā **iva** pavitrā tava kṛpā
> (Your grace is pure **like** the Gaṅgā)

**Four elements:** upameya (thing compared), upamāna (standard), sādhāraṇa-dharma (shared quality), vācaka (word of comparison).

### 2. Rūpaka (Metaphor) — "is"
Identification without comparison word. The upameya IS the upamāna.

> tava mukhaṃ candraḥ (your face IS the moon)
> saṃsāra-sāgaraḥ (saṃsāra IS an ocean)
> jñāna-dīpaḥ (knowledge IS a lamp)
> bhakti-sudhā (devotion IS nectar)
> kṛpā-varṣaḥ (grace IS rain)

### 3. Anuprāsa (Alliteration)
Repetition of consonant sounds.

> **śa**mbhuṃ **śa**ṅkaraṃ **śā**ntaṃ **śi**vam
> (alliteration of **ś**)

> **ka**ruṇā-**ka**lita-**kā**nti-**ka**malam
> (alliteration of **k**)

Types:
- **Chekānuprāsa:** same consonant cluster repeated (e.g., "nta nta")
- **Vṛttyanuprāsa:** single consonant repeated throughout

### 4. Yamaka (Rhyme/Punning Repetition)
Same syllable sequence repeated with different meanings.

> kālī kālī kālī — dark goddess / time / black
> rāma-rāma — Rāma + pleasure

### 5. Utprekṣā (Poetic Fancy/Hyperbole)
"As if" — iva, manyate, kalpyate.

> tava netraṃ padmam iva vikasati
> (your eye blooms as if it were a lotus)

### 6. Atiśayokti (Hyperbole)
Extreme exaggeration for effect.

> tava yaśaḥ diśaḥ pūrayati
> (your fame fills all directions)

### 7. Virodha (Paradox)
Apparent contradiction that reveals deeper truth.

> agninā śītalaḥ (cool with fire — Śiva drinks fire but remains cool)
> mṛtyoḥ amṛtam (from death, immortality)

### 8. Arthāntaranyāsa (Corroboration)
A general statement followed by a specific example, or vice versa.

> mahātmanāṃ prabhāvaḥ anirvacanīyaḥ / yathā śivaḥ viṣaṃ pītvā nīlakaṇṭhaḥ abhavat
> (The power of great souls is indescribable — as when Śiva drank poison and became blue-throated)

## Adorning a Simple Verse

### Without alaṅkāra:
> namāmi devīṃ sundarīṃ karuṇāmayīm
> (I bow to the beautiful, compassionate goddess)

### With upamā:
> candra-mukhīṃ devīṃ gaṅgā-sadṛśa-kṛpāṃ namāmi
> (I bow to the goddess whose face is like the moon, whose grace resembles the Gaṅgā)

### With rūpaka:
> karuṇā-sindhuṃ jñāna-dīpam devīṃ namāmi
> (I bow to the goddess who is an ocean of compassion, a lamp of knowledge)

### With anuprāsa:
> śāntāṃ śubhāṃ śivāṃ śaktiṃ devīṃ namāmi
> (alliteration of **ś** throughout)

## Milestone Check
Can you take a plain stotra verse and adorn it with at least 2 different alaṅkāras?
