## Upamā Components

| Term | Role | In: mukhaṃ candra iva śobhate |
|------|------|------|
| Upameya | thing compared | mukham (face) |
| Upamāna | standard | candra (moon) |
| Sādhāraṇa-dharma | shared quality | śobhā (beauty) |
| Vācaka | comparison word | iva (like) |

## Adornment Process

1. **Write plain verse** (correct grammar + meter)
2. **Identify plain nouns** that could become rūpakas
3. **Add iva/yathā** comparisons where natural
4. **Check for anuprāsa opportunities** — can you alliterate?
5. **Verify meter still works** after changes
