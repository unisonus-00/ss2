**Constraint analysis:**
- Forbidden "na" blocks: namas, nīla, nandī, sundara, candra, ananta, naṭarāja
- "megha" (me-gha) — no "na" ✓, can use directly
- Adbhuta rasa: use aho!, kiṃ citraṃ, kīdṛśam
- Deity epithets without "na": hara, śambhu, tryakṣa, umāpati
